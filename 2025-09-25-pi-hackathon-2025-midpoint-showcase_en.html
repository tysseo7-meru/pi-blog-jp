Pi Hackathon 2025: Midpoint Showcase and New Hackathon Ecosystem Tab and Staking

Published: September 25, 2025

Categories: hackathon

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Showing the teams' early progress at the midpoint

Pi Hackathon 2025 (a competition where teams build apps) has officially reached its midpoint. On September 19, the participating teams submitted a midpoint update. It showed how much progress they had made in the first weeks of the hackathon, and shared their ideas for the rest of the period.

To give the community an early look, Pi has released a video that introduces some of the projects being built during the hackathon. The hackathon officially ends on October 15, and only three weeks remain. Pi asks people to come back and see what the other teams have built, and how the teams shown in the video develop after that.

Being included in this showcase does not affect the final judging in any way, and it does not predict which projects will win the hackathon. The showcase is made only to provide information, to shine a light on interesting work, and to encourage the community.

Staking and rating for Hackathon 2025 begins

Together with the midpoint updates, Pi Network started a new way for Pioneers (Pi users) to take part directly in the Hackathon 2025 apps inside Pi Browser. Pioneers can now see the full list of apps submitted to the hackathon. They can stake Pi (set Pi aside to support an app) on the apps they support, which helps those apps stand out more within the system.

How to find the Hackathon 2025 apps

  • In Pi Browser, go to the PiApps tab
  • Tap Mainnet Apps (apps on the live, production network) under "Browse by category"
  • Apply the "hackathon25" filter

From there, Pioneers can find all the apps submitted to Pi Hackathon 2025. Apps will keep being submitted until October 15. Pioneers can look for projects they want to support and stake Pi on them. The amount staked on each app, and its ranking, do not affect the hackathon results. Apps are judged based on the app review criteria set out in the original hackathon announcement.

The hackathon's open theme focuses on how the Open Network's connections to the outside (the Open Network is the public network that connects Pi with the outside) expand what Pi can be used for. This means building real apps that offer practical uses, also for people who have never mined Pi. By exploring apps, staking, and rating them, the Pi community can help show which projects bring meaningful uses to the system.

This new staking and rating option extends the existing staking feature in the ecosystem directory (the list of apps inside the system). By taking part in apps now, Pioneers can help new projects come to the surface and support the builders who are creating the system.

As Pi Hackathon 2025 moves toward the final judging stage, Pi asks people to watch for further updates.
