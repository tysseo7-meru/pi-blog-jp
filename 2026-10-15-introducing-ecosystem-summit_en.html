Introducing Pi’s First Ever Ecosystem Summit: Coming November 17-19

Published: October 15, 2026

Categories: announcement, developer

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi Network will hold the Pi Ecosystem Summit (an online event for developers) for the first time. It runs for three days, from November 17 to 19, 2026. Its central themes are the developer platform, building the ecosystem (the whole system in which apps and services gather and work), and widening utility.

At the event, there will be announcements of new platforms and features, demonstrations of developer tools, introductions of apps built by partner companies and the community, discussions among developers, and a guide to a new hackathon (an event where developers compete to build apps in a short period) that starts soon.

There are two purposes. One is to show what developers have already built, what they can build from now on, and the tools available for developing and growing on Pi Network, and to get more developers to develop on Pi. The other is to let Pioneers (Pi users) learn about the apps and practical uses across the whole ecosystem.

The summit includes the following.

  • Platform releases and developer updates: announcements of new Pi platform features, functions, and materials that developers can use
  • Developer demonstrations: demonstrations of how to build apps using specific Pi tools and use the resources of the whole Pi network
  • App showcases: examples of apps built by companies and community developers, and introductions of how Pi is used and the user experience
  • Developer discussions: developers and the Pi Core Team (Pi's operating team) discuss what they want to build on Pi, platform and community support, how apps help Pi's utility, and future directions
  • A new hackathon: an introduction to a hackathon that will be a new opportunity for developers to build apps on Pi. Details will be given during the summit

Pi Day and Pi2Day are events that celebrate the progress of all Pioneers and the whole network. The Pi Ecosystem Summit differs in that it focuses narrowly on developers, the tools that support their work, and the widening of apps and Pi utility built for Pioneers.

Building on Pi

As AI lowers the barrier to building software, reaching users and being widely used is becoming an even bigger challenge for developers. Pi already has a system for delivering users and a practical ecosystem, and developers can connect their apps with more than 60 million Engaged Pioneers (actively involved users). Platform support is also in place, such as ways for apps to be discovered, user identity verification, and payments in Pi.

The ecosystem has more than 300 Mainnet (live network) apps, more than 420,000 Nodes (computers that support the network), and more than 18.1 million users who have fully completed KYC (identity verification).

Throughout 2026, Pi has widened its developer tools and materials. Recent updates include making it possible to bring outside-built AI apps into the Pi ecosystem through Pi App Studio (Pi's app creation system), the release and improvement of SoloHost, a system that lets developers publish self-run AI apps, new SDK (a set of development parts) features, and improvements to developer documentation. These lower the real barriers to building on Pi. At the Ecosystem Summit, even newer features will be introduced, and it will be shown how developers can use them to build apps and practical uses for the ecosystem.

Save the date

By joining the Pi Ecosystem Summit, held online from November 17 to 19, 2026, you can learn about new developer tools, see the apps being built across the ecosystem, and learn about the opportunities to build on Pi.

Developers who want to join the Pi ecosystem and build their own apps can look at Pi's developer documentation and start today. The detailed schedule and how to watch will be announced when the event gets closer.
