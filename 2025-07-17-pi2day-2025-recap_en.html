Pi2Day 2025 Recap

Published: July 17, 2025

Categories: pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The Pi2Day 2025 Ecosystem Challenge was held from June 28 to July 7. It celebrated and introduced the updates that support Pi2Day's various systems and usefulness.

This challenge guided Pioneers to various new features and helped them explore the Pi ecosystem and Pi products. After completing steps that had a set order, Pioneers also received fun digital prizes.

The Ecosystem Challenge

The Pi2Day Ecosystem Challenge, made of 8 steps, guided Pioneers to the new Pi2Day releases, such as Pi App Studio, Ecosystem Directory Staking, and updated Node tools. 2.6 million Pioneers started the challenge, and 761,000 people completed all the steps and obtained this year's limited-edition username badge and color.

Participation was active across regions, showing the community's engagement and willingness to explore and try new usefulness as soon as it is released.

Pi App Studio: AI app creation at a large scale

One of the big Pi2Day 2025 releases was Pi App Studio. It is an AI-supported platform that can be used without writing code, and it lets anyone, regardless of technical background, create and own apps that run within Pi's decentralized ecosystem.

Since the release of Pi App Studio, the following results were achieved.

  • More than 7,600 Chatbot apps and 14,100 Custom apps were created and published
  • Including unpublished projects, more than 34,800 individual Pioneers created apps

These apps show that grassroots innovation can grow when participation in app creation is opened without requiring a technical background.

As examples of working, useful, and fun apps created by the community, the following are listed.

  • Pi-Tris: a game where blocks fall, and you fit them well to clear lines
  • BLACKHOLE: a chatbot with information about space and celestial bodies
  • Healthy Life: a chatbot that helps Pioneers maintain their health
  • FlapPi Bird: a game where you fly a bird between two walls, aiming for a high score

Note that Pi Network states it has never, for any reason or purpose, verified, evaluated, recommended, or reviewed the App Studio apps available through Pi Browser, and it does not do so now. Use of these apps is at your own responsibility, and great care when using them is strongly recommended.

Ecosystem Directory Staking: supporting Pi apps and usefulness

Another Pi2Day 2025 release was Ecosystem Directory Staking. This is a platform-level usefulness that lets businesses and users raise the ranking of Pi apps in the Ecosystem Interface by staking Pi tokens (setting Pi aside to support them) on the Mainnet (production network) blockchain.

  • In total, more than 37.7 million Pi were staked, and more than 25 million Pi are still actively staked
  • More than 16,700 individual Pioneers staked across more than 1,450 separate apps

The staking feature introduced a community-led way for Pioneers and businesses to bring forward and support the apps and services they want to spread and support.

A community supported by usefulness

Thanks are expressed to all the Pioneers who explored new features, created apps, shared apps on Fireside Forum, and staked Pi to support the ecosystem. This year's challenge reflected the community's continuing interest in contributing to the growth of the Pi ecosystem and the expansion of its usefulness.

With thousands of apps created, millions of Pi staked, and active worldwide participation in the Ecosystem Challenge, Pi2Day 2025 showed that new tools and efforts are being actively used by the community. These results also show the Pioneers' continuing role: using and trying products, providing feedback, and supporting the development of the ecosystem through use and participation.
