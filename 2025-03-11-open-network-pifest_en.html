PiFest is Back: Celebrating Pi Commerce in the Open Network Era!

Published: March 11, 2025

Categories: announcement, pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

PiFest is back. After the start of the Open Network (the live network that connects with the outside), Pi builds on the big success of the previous PiFest in 2024. In the previous PiFest, there was engagement in local Pi commerce around the world at a level never seen before. There were more than 55,000 sellers (27,000 of them were active sellers in more than 160 countries) and more than 950,000 Pioneers took part over a few days. Past PiFests in the Enclosed Period (the period when the network was not connected to the outside) already showed that the use of Pi in local commerce is not just an idea. It is an active reality.

Now, external connection through the Open Network is working. PiFest continues to support commerce connected with Pi. It gives even bigger chances for merchants to join, for Pioneers to take part, and for smooth transactions on the Pi Mainnet blockchain (the live blockchain) and on related Pi products.

Whether you are a merchant who wants to reach new customers, or a Pioneer who wants to explore the growing world of shopping with Pi, PiFest is a chance to take part.

Merchant registration and the event schedule are as follows.

  • Merchant registration opens: Wednesday, March 12, 2025
  • PiFest begins: Pi Day, March 14, 2025
  • PiFest ends: Friday, March 21, 2025

Merchants can register their businesses on Map of Pi. Map of Pi is an app made by community developers. It won the Pi Commerce Hackathon (a contest where people build apps in a short time). It lets Pioneers find local businesses that accept Pi and connect with them.

During PiFest, all members of the Pi Network community can take part by shopping with Pi, engaging with local businesses, and sharing their experiences on Fireside Forum and on social media.

How PiFest Works

The flow is: discover, shop, and share.

From small businesses to large service providers, PiFest continues to bring communities together. It gives Pioneers the power to engage in real Pi commerce and to explore uses for Pi in the community.

Merchants can list their businesses on Map of Pi, so Pioneers can find them. Pioneers can explore local businesses that accept Pi, search by type or place, and leave reviews. Transactions take place in the store. Merchants can easily show their Pi Wallet QR code (a square code that can be scanned to pay), and Pioneers can pay safely with Pi on the Pi Mainnet blockchain.

For local commerce, PiFest uses the full Pi ecosystem (all the related apps and services together). This includes payments with Pi Wallet, transactions on the Pi Mainnet blockchain, and social sharing on Fireside Forum. All of these work smoothly together across different existing Pi apps. With these tools and this connected experience, Pioneers and merchants can take part in transactions with Pi through Pi's active ecosystem.

To celebrate, participants are encouraged to share their experiences on Fireside Forum (#PiFest) and on social media, using #PiFest. Good Fireside Forum posts may be shown on Pi's official channels.

Guidelines for Merchants and Pioneers

Guidelines for merchants and for Pioneers are given. The details for each group follow under the headings below.

For Merchants

By March 14, 2025, do the following.

  • List your business, choose your seller type, and register on Map of Pi
  • Print and display your Pi Wallet QR code for easy transactions (wallet.pinet.com → Pay/Request → Share → Print)
  • Promote your store on Fireside Forum (#PiFest) and on social media, and let Pioneers know that you accept Pi

During PiFest, do the following.

  • Engage with shoppers, answer questions, and encourage reviews

For Pioneers

  • Find businesses on Map of Pi, or on other ecosystem apps that support local commerce, and plan your shopping visits
  • Shop with Pi Wallet at participating merchants
  • Leave a review on Map of Pi to support merchants and build trust
  • Share your experience on Fireside Forum and on social media using #PiFest, for a chance to be featured

You can see a step-by-step guide on how to use Map of Pi in the linked video.
