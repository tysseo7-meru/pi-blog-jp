Pi2Day 2026 Recap

Published: August 5, 2026

Categories: pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The Pi2Day 2026 Ecosystem Quest was held from June 28 to July 13, 2026. It celebrated and introduced the major ecosystem and usefulness-supporting updates of Pi2Day.

The Quest guided Pioneers to complete activities such as finding new apps, trying ecosystem features, and understanding how Pi's foundation (infrastructure) supports usefulness creation in the areas of computing, AI, and identity verification. Pioneers who completed the Quest also received a special-edition in-app badge that can be used in Pi Chats and Pi Social Profiles.

The Ecosystem Quest

The Pi2Day Ecosystem Quest let Pioneers experience, in order, the Pi ecosystem features and the new Pi2Day releases. More than 2.56 million Pioneers started the Quest, and more than 1.78 million Pioneers completed all the steps and received this year's special-edition badge.

The steps included the following.

  • Explore SoloHost apps in Pi Desktop: it was shown how Node operators and Pi Desktop users can run and manage SoloHost applications, including local AI agents (AI assistants that run on your own computer) and other tools that go beyond blockchain verification
  • Take part in staking to the Ecosystem Directory: Pioneers touched the renewed ecosystem screen (interface) and experienced how to stake Pi (set Pi aside to support) to support apps and make them more visible
  • Find apps made with vibe coding: Pioneers explored apps made with external AI tools and Pi App Studio, staked Pi to support their ranking on the ecosystem screen, and shared their favorite apps in the Vibe Code channel of Fireside Forum

The strong participation showed the community's engagement and willingness to learn about, explore, and try new practical systems each time they are released.

The main Pi2Day releases

Pi Network made three new releases for Pi2Day. These widen Pi's usefulness inside the Pi ecosystem, and also widen it to the world outside the Pi platform. At the same time, they invite outside businesses and developers to join Pi Network and use Pi's unique advantages and resources.

SoloHost is an open, permissionless framework (a basic structure anyone can join without approval) on Pi Desktop. Developers can build and list apps that help run local AI, and apps that will soon be usable for distributed computing (splitting computing among several computers) purposes. Through the early beta (trial version) of SoloHost, which is being released in stages, users can find and run these apps on their own computers, and use the corresponding apps from a mobile device on Pi Browser. Community developers have already published 110 apps through SoloHost, showing developers' early interest in building practical systems that reach more than 420,000 Pi Node operators, and even more Pi Desktop users who can run apps on their own devices.

Pi Sign-in lets Pioneers sign in (log in) with their Pi account to supported third-party websites and apps. Even outside Pi Browser, third parties can receive the benefit of Pi's network scale and its verified identity resources. This widens Pi's presence across the broader internet and third-party settings, makes integration easier, and makes the connection with the Pi ecosystem smoother.

PiVerify provides Pi's ability to confirm that someone is a real human being to third-party clients who need KYC (identity verification procedures) and identity verification for their own users. Through this, outside platforms gain a means to reduce fake and duplicate accounts and to support work related to legal compliance. At the same time, by verifying those users, the total population of individuals verified through Pi's KYC system grows. Clients pay in Pi for using the PiVerify service, so Pi's usefulness also increases.

Taken together, Pi's usefulness advances in the areas of computing, AI, and identity verification. By providing useful services and resources that first help outside third parties — such as Pi's blockchain foundation, identity verification, and a large, globally engaged network — Pi presents several unique motivations not only to use Pi's services, but also to join and contribute to Pi's existing ecosystem.

Details of each release can be read in the full Pi2Day 2026 blog.

A community moved by usefulness

Thank you to all the Pioneers who took part in the Ecosystem Quest. The Quest provided the community with a hands-on way to engage with the Pi2Day releases and to explore how new tools, applications, and foundations are widening usefulness across the whole Pi ecosystem.
