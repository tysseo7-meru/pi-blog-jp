Pi Founders to Speak at Consensus Miami 2026

Published: April 23, 2026

Categories: general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The two founders of Pi Network are introduced as speakers at Consensus Miami.

Together, the two talks show Pi's way of thinking about the age of AI (artificial intelligence). One part is to support products centered on real uses and business models that can last a long time, through blockchain (a technology that keeps records of transactions spread across many computers, so the records are hard to change), confirmed identity information, and a network that is active around the world. The other part is to make identity checks at a world scale possible through Pi's own KYC system (a process to check who a person is), and to provide solutions for proving that something is real.

Chengdiao Fan will speak on Wednesday, May 6, from 11:15 to 11:35 (EDT, Eastern Daylight Time in the United States), on the Convergence Stage at Consensus 2026.

As AI greatly lowers the difficulty of building products, the center of competition is moving to real data, gaining users, and trusted human participation.

In Chengdiao's talk, 'Aligning Web3, AI, and Blockchain for Utility,' the talk will look at how Pi's blockchain base, confirmed identity information, and network active around the world can support products centered on real uses and business models for the age of AI.

This talk shows a new way to see crypto tokens (units of digital value issued on a blockchain). It sees them not as an exit for selling, but as tools for long-lasting growth and spread in the real world.

Nicolas Kokkalis will speak in a panel discussion (a discussion with several speakers) on Thursday, May 7, from 10:15 to 10:45 (EDT), on the Convergence Stage at Consensus 2026.

Pi is working on one of the urgent problems of the age of AI: the problem of proving that a person online is a real human.

The session 'How to Prove You're Human in an AI World (Without Doxing Yourself)' will look at how the trust system of the internet is breaking down. This is happening because AI systems can now create profiles and create bots (programs that act automatically) that behave like real users.

At the end, the original article invites people who want to know more to check Consensus 2026, which will be held in Miami on May 6 and May 7.
