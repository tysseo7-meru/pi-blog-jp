Pi Ad Network Expansion: Now Open to All Ecosystem Listed Pi Apps

Published: April 10, 2025

Categories: announcement, developer

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Last year, Pi introduced its plan for the Pi Ad Network and its first test run. In this network, advertisers use Pi to place ads in the Pi ecosystem (the group of apps and services around Pi) and in Pi apps. At first, it was released as a test to 5 community apps. It was improved again and again together with the developers of those apps. The ad network is the first concrete example of a basic-level use that uses the shared resources of Pi Network to give the Pi cryptocurrency real and natural ways to be used.

Developers can now apply to join the Pi Ad Network. They can join the first group of Pi apps that took part in the test program. Those apps are now earning and receiving ad income. However, to be chosen, an app must be listed on the Mainnet Ecosystem screen and must follow the developer ecosystem guidelines. Also, applying does not guarantee that an app will be included. To be approved, an app must meet the requirements for listing in the Mainnet ecosystem.

This timing is a turning point for Pi's basic-level uses. It turns the shared attention of Pi's millions of active Pioneers (Pi users) into a visible, lasting, and inclusive (open to many people without leaving anyone out) system that supports the Pi app ecosystem.

What the Pi Ad Network Is For

As shown in earlier articles about basic-level uses and the test release of the Pi Ad Network, this work was designed to turn Pi's shared resources into a form of use that helps developers and Pioneers across the whole network. These shared resources include the attention of Pioneers and the size and depth of involvement of the community.

Benefits for Developers

During the Open Network period (the period of the live network that can connect to outside systems), the Pi Ad Network lets Pi app developers earn money from the attention of Pioneers. Their apps attract this attention from the base of Pi through the Pi Browser.

Building and keeping a meaningful app takes resources. A meaningful app is an app that attracts Pioneers, keeps them, and meets their needs. It needs time, skill, basic equipment and systems, and ongoing work to run it. As use of an app grows, the costs that income from the Pi Ad Network can cover also grow.

The Pi Ad Network solves this problem. It lets developers earn income directly in Pi, in line with the real attention their apps create across the whole base. Advertisers must get and use Pi to reach this attention. In this way, the network creates a system that goes in a circle. Developers are paid in the same currency that their users hold and use. This gives them a reason to improve app quality, keep developing, take part in the ecosystem, and create new ideas over the long term. In this way, the problem of earning money becomes a chance to create uses. It supports a healthier app ecosystem and wider uses for Pi itself.

Benefits for Pioneers

The Pi Ad Network requires Pi to be used for ad deals shown in the ecosystem. This makes sure that access to the shared resource of attention in the ecosystem depends on the Pi cryptocurrency that every Pioneer holds. This makes sure that the benefits for developers match the benefits for the whole community.

For example, developers who are supported by income from the ad network can build and create more Pi apps. In those apps, Pioneers can use Pi for goods and services. At the network level, this moves the whole ecosystem and the creation of uses forward. In the end, it benefits the whole Pi Network.

Guide for Developers: Apply to Join the Pi Ad Network

Pi is now opening the Pi Ad Network SDK (a full set of tools for development) to all community developers whose apps are on the Mainnet Ecosystem screen. Developers who can take part can add ads to their Pi apps through one shared framework, in a way that is consistent, safe, and easy for users to understand. Documents, technical guides for getting started, and guidelines for ad content are available in the Pi Developer Portal (the entry point for developers).

A simple guide to sending an application through the Developer Portal is as follows.

  • 1. Open the Pi Browser and choose Develop to go to the Developer Portal.
  • 2. In the Developer Portal, choose the app you want to apply for the Pi Ad Network.
  • 3. Scroll down on the app details page and press the application button for Dev Ad Network.
  • 4. Click Ads Checklist and complete all 3 steps shown in the Pi Ad Platform Checklist. Follow the instructions carefully. When you finish, send the application form.
