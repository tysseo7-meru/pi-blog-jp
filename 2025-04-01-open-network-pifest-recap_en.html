PiFest Recap: Advancing Local Commerce Utility in Open Network

Published: April 1, 2025

Categories: general, pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The first PiFest in the Open Network (the live network connected to the outside) for Pi Network has ended, with historic global engagement. It showed the growing momentum of local commerce powered by Pi, and the widening utility of Pi in everyday transactions. PiFest 2025, held from March 14 (Pi Day) to March 21, 2025, was the first global commerce event fully supported by the Open Network's external connectivity, and it set a new standard for large-scale, real-world Pi utility.

Large-scale participation reflects ecosystem growth

This year's PiFest brought together Pioneers and local shops around the world. Using the Open Network's connectivity, it widened the spread of digital transactions in local commerce through the Pi Mainnet (live network) blockchain.

Registered sellers passed 125,000, of whom 58,000 were active sellers. The event showed a large expansion from the previous year and a clear upward trend in Pi adoption in local commerce. More than 1.8 million Pioneers used the Map of Pi (an app for finding shops that accept Pi on a map), and more than 45,000 reviews (written evaluations by users) were submitted by Pioneers. This helped build trust and visibility within the community-driven Pi commerce ecosystem (the set of Pi-related apps and services).

Pioneers took part in real transactions, from daily goods to specialized services, reflecting Pi's widening reach and practical use. The event saw participation from a diverse range of businesses — cozy cafes, fashion boutiques (small clothing shops), car repair shops, freelance designers (people who work without belonging to an organization), and more — each using the Pi Wallet for payment and taking in Pi as a payment method.

Being able to do all of this within the Pi ecosystem — finding local businesses through the Map of Pi, paying through the Pi Wallet and the Pi Mainnet blockchain, and engaging through Fireside Forum (Pi's social app) — reflects again that Pi's circular loop of utility is working.

PiFest is a vehicle for speeding up utility in the Open Network

As was stressed throughout the event, PiFest is not just a celebration. It shows Pi's real-world utility through concrete examples. With the Open Network fully launched, PiFest shows that Pi can support real commerce and back local economies around the world.

The event marked an important moment in Pi's journey: the move from utility in the Enclosed Network (the network of the period not connected to the outside), which was limited to inside the Pi network, to large-scale practical utility in the world-open Open Network. The success of PiFest showed that Pioneers and businesses are open to, and eager for, transacting in Pi. It strengthens Pi Network's long-term vision of building an inclusive (welcoming many people without distinction) peer-to-peer ecosystem powered by Pi.

PiFest has ended, but the ecosystem it widens keeps growing. Pioneers are encouraged to keep looking for businesses connected with Pi, through the Map of Pi and other Pi apps. Shops can register their own stores and keep reaching new customers using Pi.
