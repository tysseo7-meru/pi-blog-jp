Pi Ecosystem Summit Recap: New Tools, App Growth Support, and Pi Next

Published: November 24, 2026

Categories: developer, general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi Network's first-ever Ecosystem Summit was held on November 17-19, 2026. It introduced new developer tools, efforts to support app growth, and Pi Next, a completely new hackathon (an event where people compete to develop intensively in a short period) with a total prize pool of 200,000 USDT. The program combined announcements with technical demonstrations and conversations about how developers can make use of Pi's base and its global community.

The full announcements are in the Day 1 and Day 2 blog articles, and the videos can be watched on the Ecosystem Summit webpage.

How to take part in Pi Next, and its requirements, are on the hackathon webpage. Final submissions are due January 5, 2027, at 11:59 PM PST (Pacific Standard Time). Pioneers (Pi users) can also share the opportunity with other developers and relevant communities.

Below is a quick recap of the key updates.

Featured tools for building and growing applications

  • New Developer Web Console and MCP: Compatible AI coding agents (AI that receives instructions and carries out programming work) can help developers register and configure apps and integrate Pi authentication and Pi payments. Founder Nicolas Kokkalis demonstrated the process by building an app from scratch.
  • Up to 20 million Pi for app growth: The App Growth Initiative, sponsored by the Pi Foundation, introduces a marketing budget of up to 20 million Pi. Eligible apps can benefit from platform-supported incentives (rewards supported by the platform) that reward eligible KYC-verified (identity-verified) Pioneers for qualifying app participation and referrals. Developers can attract and engage users without funding those rewards themselves.
  • SoloHost AI packaging: Developers can use an AI agent to prepare an existing application's public Docker image (a distribution format that bundles an app together with the environment it needs to run) for distribution through Pi Desktop. The agent generates and validates the required configuration files.
  • Developer incentives and a redesigned Pi App Directory: New discovery sections highlight apps based on user interest, growth, and repeat engagement, alongside the existing community staking-based ranking (a ranking based on community members staking Pi to support apps).
  • On-chain subscriptions on Testnet: Users can authorize recurring payments within a defined budget and period, without signing every renewal. Funds remain in their wallets until each payment is processed.
  • x402 payments on Testnet: OpenMind's RoboPay demo showed a Test-Pi payment followed by delivery of a service command, demonstrating programmatic payments (payments carried out by programs) for applications, AI agents, and services.
  • Pi integrations: The CiDi Games demo showcases the use of Pi user authentication (checking who a user is), Pi Payments for transactions within supported gaming experiences, and the Pi Ads SDK (a development kit for putting ads into an app) for integrating monetization through advertisements into applications.
  • Notification Center: Developers can integrate notifications delivered within Pi Browser to keep users informed about relevant app activity and encourage them to return.
  • Launchpad, DEX, and AMM: Mainnet in Q1 2027 (details in the next section).

Launchpad, DEX, and AMM: Mainnet in Q1 2027

Pi announced that Pi Launchpad, decentralized exchange (DEX, an exchange where users trade directly without a central operator), and automated market maker (AMM, a system that sets prices automatically to make trades happen) capabilities will launch on Mainnet in Q1 2027. These capabilities will provide infrastructure for ecosystem token launches and applications that incorporate tokens for specific utility purposes.

Pi DataLab and developer showcases

Pi DataLab is a new human-in-the-loop platform (a way of working where human judgment is included in the process of AI work) designed to connect organizations needing human input with Pi's identity-verified community. Through Pi DataLab, organizations will be able to create and fund projects requiring human input, including data labeling (adding labels to data), ranking, evaluation, physical AI data (AI data collected in the real world), and other tasks that rely on human judgment. Eligible Pioneers will be able to complete tasks and receive Pi or other supported tokens.

The DataLab preview demonstrated project setup, task completion, and quality controls for work.

Developer discussions with Pi Founder Chengdiao Fan

Pi Founder Chengdiao Fan was joined by Pi app developers CiDi Games and Resonant for discussions about building on Pi. The CiDi Games conversation explored overall Pi ecosystem strategies, gaming utility, app growth, and developer support. The Resonant conversation focused on human-generated data for AI and opportunities for developers and Pioneers to participate in the AI economy.

Other general updates

  • Improved onboarding and deep linking (links that take a user directly to a specific place inside an app): Users opening a PiNet app link (pinet.com) can install Pi Browser and then be directed to the original app or page they intended to visit on Pi Browser, reducing user onboarding friction (the trouble of getting started) for apps in the Pi ecosystem.
  • Expanded technical language and framework support (structures for development): Developer tools now support Laravel, Go, FastAPI, Node/Express, and Ruby.
  • Unified platform documentation: App setup, authentication, payments, and testing guidance are now brought together in one place.

Get started with Pi's developer tools

The Ecosystem Summit highlighted new ways to build applications, reach users, and expand utility across Pi's ecosystem. Explore the Summit's announcements and videos to learn more, and join Pi Next to put Pi's developer tools and resources into practice.
