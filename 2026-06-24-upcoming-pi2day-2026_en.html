Contribute to Pi’s Ecosystem Ahead of Pi2Day 2026 with Vibe Coder Campaign and Launchpad Testnet Token

Published: June 24, 2026

Categories: general, pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

With Pi2Day 2026 approaching, Pioneers (people who use Pi) still have time to take part in two ongoing efforts, both ending on Sunday, June 28.

  • Join the Vibe Coder campaign (an effort to invite people who build apps with AI to Pi) and its raffle, and help bring more AI-created apps to Pi.
  • Try the testnet token SLICE on Pi Launchpad (Pi's system for apps to launch tokens), together with an updated participation flow.

Pi2Day has historically been an important time for milestones, updates, and new feature announcements in the Pi Network ecosystem. Last year, projects around AI, Pi App Studio, and ecosystem participation, as well as major platform-level tools, were introduced at Pi2Day.

The Vibe Coder campaign: bring more AI-created apps to Pi

Pioneers have four days left to take part in the Vibe Coder campaign. They can help bring more AI-created apps to Pi, and by introducing vibe coders (people who build apps with AI) to Pi's real distribution network and utility ecosystem through Pi App Studio, they can enter a raffle to win Pi merchandise, including Pi Network jackets and sweaters.

Pioneers can take part by joining relevant online creator and developer communities on other social media platforms, contributing naturally within those communities, and sharing posts that explain how the Pi ecosystem can help creators who have already built prototypes or working apps on AI platforms, through app distribution and monetization opportunities.

In their posts, they can point vibe coders to Pi App Studio, explain how easily apps built outside can connect with Pi, and highlight that Pi provides access to the ecosystem's 60M+ (more than 60 million) active users, together with infrastructure that has payments, identity verification, and advertising built in.

After that, by submitting the link to their post through the "Vibe Coder" button in the Pi mining app, they can enter the raffle for Pi Network merchandise.

The original article points readers to the official rules and details.

By helping more creators discover Pi, Pioneers help widen the range of apps, tools, and services available in the Pi ecosystem. As the app ecosystem grows stronger, Pioneers get more ways to use Pi, ways to support useful products, and ways to take part in utility.

Try the SLICE testnet token and the updated flow on Pi Launchpad

Pi Launchpad recently released SLICE, its second testnet token, together with an improved participation flow and an updated model.

This second test token release is designed to address issues found in the first test release, and to help Pioneers learn how Launchpad works in a safe testnet environment. SLICE will never move to Mainnet. It also helps Pi collect useful data and community feedback to further improve the Launchpad product before real Mainnet token launches.

Pioneers were encouraged to try the SLICE test launch, use the connected Slice of Pi app, check the updated participation flow, and share feedback that helps improve the updated Launchpad system.

How to take part is as follows.

  • Open Pi Browser and go to the Pi Launchpad app.
  • Check the test token and the issuing project. Before deciding to take part, try using the Slice of Pi app. Using it will help you judge whether the product is useful and whether it is worth taking part in the token launch.
  • Choose a participation amount in Test-Pi (Pi for testing) and complete the payment flow to confirm your participation. Launchpad automatically calculates the related fair-access hold (Pi set aside temporarily to keep participation fair), and shows the participation amount, the hold amount, and the total before you confirm.
  • Use the Slice of Pi app before and after participating. Depending on how much you use it during the launch period, you may qualify for a percentage bonus on the SLICE tokens you receive. Your use also helps the project gather user feedback and confirm its use cases.

Get involved with the Pi ecosystem and help it grow before Pi2Day

Pi2Day is a time to get involved with new product announcements and gain an understanding of the network's direction and priorities. This year, before the June 28 event, there is also a timely opportunity for Pioneers to take part in expanding app use cases, community growth, and feedback on the ecosystem's development.

Readers were called on to join both efforts now and wait for Pi2Day.
