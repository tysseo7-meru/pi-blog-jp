Pi2Day 2025: Launching AI Initiative to Scale Next-Generation App Creation and Platform Utilities

Published: June 27, 2025

Categories: announcement, pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The article begins with the greeting "Happy Pi2Day 2025".

On this day, Pi released two major features. (1) An AI app platform that tries to solve, based on both blockchain and AI technology, the problems the world faces as AI advances, and that widens the opportunities for Pioneers to contribute to, engage with, and support Pi apps and usefulness; and (2) another platform-level usefulness that lets Pioneers and businesses support Pi apps and usefulness through a staking (setting aside) system.

In addition, various technology and product updates were released, and a new Pi2Day Ecosystem Challenge also began. This challenge guides Pioneers to the new releases and the functions of the Pi ecosystem, and digital prizes can be obtained.

Before describing the Pi2Day releases, the article digs into a question about AI, blockchain, and Pi Network that was put to the community earlier this week: "How does generative AI relate to Pi Network?".

Pi's solution for a world where AI advances

The ultimate usefulness of any technology, including blockchain, lies in solving humanity's fundamental problems. At present, the AI foundation is at an important point where breakthrough progress is happening. However, the world still faces the following unsolved problems.

  • The immaturity of the AI app layer, which lies between the AI foundation and actual production
  • The lack of clarity about the role of human labor and intelligence
  • The unsolved question of human well-being in a world where AI is widespread, where AI can produce an unprecedented amount of production, and how the resulting benefits will be distributed to humans in a form that keeps, or improves, acceptable levels of equality and fairness

Pi believes that by using both AI and blockchain technology, the world can solve these problems. In Pi's view, the meeting of the two technologies extends beyond the typical understanding of "decentralized AI apps". That understanding often focuses narrowly on distributed data, computing, governance, and ownership for AI accountability. These aspects are also important, but Pi Network sees, as a more fundamental usefulness of blockchain, a scalable and accessible solution for social distribution and redistribution. Pi therefore emphasizes a broader outlook on blockchain and AI: namely, distributing, at a macro level through blockchain, the production that AI creates. Through blockchain technology, the production created by AI should be distributed fairly and inclusively among humans within a crypto network, addressing the core issues around human well-being, economic fairness, and a sustainable role for human intelligence in an AI-led future.

To realize this outlook, several important elements are needed. First, authenticity must be established, real humans and AI beings must be accurately told apart, and distribution must be made to reach real humans. Second, a thriving and rich AI app layer is needed, connecting advanced AI foundations with practical real-world production, and creators must be able to easily build innovative AI apps combined with blockchain. Third, a lively social network is essential, to distribute these apps widely, let many people actually use them, and enable sustainable business forms. Finally, by using blockchain's native features of accountability and transparent rights management, it can be made certain that the production these apps create brings benefits directly and fairly to real human users within the ecosystem.

Pi Network is said to have already established the necessary important foundation elements, and to be in a unique position to address these challenges effectively. On authenticity, Pi developed its own KYC (identity verification) system, requires all accounts to pass identity verification before taking part in the Pi Mainnet (production network), and ensures a high level of humanness in the community. At present, after identity verification has been processed through Pi KYC, more than 13.7 million people have migrated to the Pi Mainnet. To meet the need for a large social network, Pi has one of the world's largest crypto-ready social networks, with more than 60 million active members in more than 200 countries and regions.

On blockchain's accountability and transparency features, Pi began as a blockchain project, used fair design principles in its mining system, and made the network's benefits wide and accessible, shared inclusively by the community. Finally, to encourage a thriving and rich AI app layer, on this day Pi officially released Pi App Studio, which uses the power of generative AI, so that not only engineers but also creators can easily build and publish innovative AI apps combined with blockchain. Supporting Pi App Studio, in addition to existing Pi products such as the Pi Ad Network, .pi Domains, and Pi Wallet, the Ecosystem Directory Staking feature released on this day will further enable the actual use of these apps and help establish sustainable business forms within the Pi ecosystem.

Pi App Studio: a new form of app creation in the AI era

Pi App Studio is one attempt to realize a vision shared by many engineers: a world where anyone, without needing a technical background, can build and run apps using human language. In addition to the power of generative AI, important aspects that separate Pi App Studio from other current generative AI vibe coding platforms (platforms where the AI writes the program based on your instructions in words) are the guided app creation process, the millions of potential creators, and access to the users of these generated apps, providing an efficient improvement process considered necessary for real product creation. For that reason, Pi App Studio does not only build and run apps; together with the other existing elements of the Pi ecosystem, it enables the creation of real, working online products.

The reason a guided process is needed is based on Pi's insight that not everyone has the expertise to build products. To let anyone build products, solving only the technical barriers to entry is not enough; that is only half of the problem. With the rise of AI, the constraints in app creation have shifted from technical skill to creativity and distribution — that is, what to build and where to publish it.

A guided process is essential for ordinary people to draw out and combine their own field knowledge, expertise, and talent, and turn it into a real product that helps others. To explain with an analogy: even if you give a blank canvas and brushes to a person who has never learned painting, they have the necessary tools, but they may not be able to paint a good picture. To produce a beautiful picture that takes in that person's unique viewpoint and creativity, an educational, guided process is needed, providing the structure required for that person's work.

New products need users who try them and provide feedback. Through this, features and business forms can be further refined and improved, and the product's sustainability can be ensured. That is exactly what the existing social network and ecosystem elements of Pi Network can provide to app creators. App creators can get real feedback on their products from existing Pioneers, and with the help of current Pi products such as the Pi Ad Network, .pi Domains, and Pi Wallet, they can build a business form on Pi. Conversely, the Pi ecosystem benefits from large-scale app creation by creators, expansion of usefulness, and community engagement.

The significance of this AI effort is said to be three things.

  • It is a contribution to the world's AI app layer. Pi Network innovates with a guided app creation process on top of the world's recent AI foundation progress, and uses the creativity of the Pi community crowd and the distribution channels of Pi's social network
  • It is an experiment in exploring, using blockchain, a form of economy where human intelligence and AI coexist sustainably. This is an essential worldwide problem to solve in the coming AI era
  • It scales up the grassroots power of Pioneers without technical skills to use their own expertise, innovate with AI and blockchain, build apps, and develop the ecosystem's usefulness

Through this, app creation improvement and attempts by a wider range of people will increase, in a distributed form, at lower cost than the technical cost required to build apps in the Web2 era.

By combining AI with Pi Network's tools and collective resources, Pi App Studio is positioned to help people turn their own knowledge into real apps that others can use and benefit from.

The version released on this day includes two options. The first is a process in which Pioneers can create topic-specific chatbots, trained and automated by AI, using the creator's field knowledge and expertise to polish a generative AI chatbot so that it answers users' questions about a specific topic better, and so that the user experience can be adjusted. A general chatbot can also provide answers, but a polished AI chatbot that uses each creator's field expertise and creativity can provide more tailored answers and diverse user experiences, and may extend beyond the conventional chatbot screen. There is no reason why question-and-answer exchanges must always be in the form of a "chatbot"; they could also be in playful forms such as a step-by-step wizard, an interactive quiz, a recommendation system, or a Magic 8 Ball. Furthermore, the great majority of general users, beyond the layer of people familiar with technology and AI, do not yet rely on general generative AI chatbots for their daily information needs. To accelerate adoption by the general public and raise human productivity, more targeted, topic-specific AI apps, infused with each creator's expertise and creativity, can become a more intuitive and effective entry point, and may encourage wider engagement with AI.

The second option is a beta, open platform that lets Pioneers build any kind of app they already have in mind, without needing to write code. Pioneers can build and own the apps they want to build using human language, not code. Because it is still in beta, access may be limited, and the user experience is still being polished, as noted.

As the number of processes built on the current platform grows, more kinds of app creation will be able to be guided.

When a creator's chatbot, other kinds of apps, or custom apps are completed, Pi App Studio uses AI to automatically deploy the app to a cloud server and helps publish their app to the Pi ecosystem. Based on user feedback, creators can repeatedly judge what resonates with Pioneers, apply changes to the app in a process similar to the creation flow, redeploy it, and republish it to the community. All of this can be done through Pi App Studio without writing code.

As with other products, to increase user participation and engagement, Pi App Studio creators can also use other Pi products, such as Fireside Forum and Pi Chats, to connect their apps with the wider Pioneer community. To build a business form on Pi, creators can later use the Pi Ad Network, through which Pi app owners currently earn revenue, and can currently also bid for a .pi Domain for ease of access to their app.

Pi Network's unique strengths, such as the tens of millions of Pioneers who have completed identity verification holding and using the Pi cryptocurrency, give app creators motivation to prioritize quality, growth, and accuracy. The better an app is made, the more likely people are to use it.

Building an app through Pi App Studio is said to mean that you can own and run a business and a product built around your own knowledge and ideas, made possible by the use of AI, and supported by Pi's blockchain ecosystem.

Ecosystem Directory Staking: using Pi to support Pi apps and usefulness

Ecosystem Directory Staking is a new platform-level usefulness. It introduces a distributed way for Pioneers and businesses to actively support and spread the ranking of Pi apps and usefulness in the Ecosystem Interface. Available through Pi Browser, with rollout beginning June 28, this feature lets Pioneers and businesses stake Pi for an app of their choice on the Mainnet blockchain, directly raising that app's ranking within the ecosystem. In addition to Pi's usefulness in staking, this feature also aims to provide greater exposure to apps that provide higher quality and more meaningful community engagement.

As a June 29, 2025 update, staking is described as a voluntary way for Pioneers and businesses to support apps they use and enjoy in the Ecosystem Interface. Through this staking feature, it would make no sense for the network to push a specific Pi app over other apps, so at the protocol level (the level of the rules for communication and processing) there is no Pi reward. Whether a third-party developer of a specific Pi app or service provides some incentive — such as app features and quality, user experience, in-app rewards, or promotion — to have Pioneers stake Pi for their app or service and raise its ranking within the ecosystem, is up to that developer. The original amount staked is returned to the user when the staking period ends.

Strategically, this system is a platform-level usefulness that uses the resource of Pi's collective attention. It not only supports apps and usefulness with the power of the Pi community crowd, but also draws a line from the typical Web2 app ranking systems, where rigid algorithms create information isolation or which are heavily controlled by advertising companies. This feature encourages a cycle designed to raise quality and distribute influence across the whole Pi Network.

Integration with the third-party Onramper and on-ramp services

The Pi Wallet is now integrated with Onramper. Onramper is a third-party business that brings together on-ramps (entry points for exchanging fiat currency and the like into crypto assets) that have completed KYB (business verification), and it simplifies on-ramp services for Pioneers. Pioneers can access Onramper through the Pi Wallet and, subject to regional availability and other restrictions, choose the KYB-compliant on-ramp service they prefer. This integration provides more Pioneers with a simplified way to take part in the Mainnet ecosystem. Ease of use will be widened in stages over the coming week.

Node updates: Node Version 0.5.2 and the Node ranking page

Node Version 0.5.2 has been released, and it includes several important updates.

  • The app's name was changed to Pi Desktop, so that it includes both Pi Node and the Pi mining app. With this change, the app becomes more general and accurate, and opens to current and future desktop integrations not necessarily related to Nodes. For example, accessing Pi App Studio on a large computer screen (to open App Studio in Pi Desktop, choose Pi Utilities in the Ξ hamburger menu of the Pi App tab)
  • Security was improved overall
  • Pioneers can view the public key directly in the Node entry, to identify their Node on the Node ranking page. This public key can be used to confirm and identify the user's Node
  • The desktop app's window can now be resized, improving ease of use in some features such as Pi App Studio

The new Node ranking page of Pi Blockexplorer displays the top 5,000 Pi Nodes, based on key performance indicators such as reliability, availability, open ports, total active days, and CPU performance. The ranking is updated every 24 hours to reflect the latest data from the network. This page helps highlight the most stable and highest-performing nodes that contribute to the Pi ecosystem.

Mainnet migration and KYC updates: removing the obstacles for more than 500,000 additional Pioneers

With recent updates on Mainnet migration and KYC, more Pioneers have become able to be eligible for migration. Through these updates, the obstacles were removed for more than 500,000 additional Pioneers who previously could not proceed. This is in addition to the usual migration flow, which has migrated more than 3 million Pioneers since the start of the Open Network.

One KYC update helped Pioneers in specific regions, who had unique challenges requiring further verification, to remove obstacles so they could reach the final KYC state from the Tentative KYC (provisional identity verification) state and continue the Mainnet migration process.

The migration process was also improved by having a dedicated blockchain API server, kept separate from other blockchain communication. This helped improve the stability and throughput of migration.

Pioneers may need to complete specific actions before migration happens. The exact timing depends on when each individual finishes those steps, but they are no longer limited by the system restrictions that previously blocked migration.

Extension of the .pi Domains Auction

As discussed in an earlier article, .pi Domains are intended to be functional Web3 identifiers, that is, a home for apps, services, and stores that run on Pi. Because Pi App Studio, which lets far more Pioneers build and run their own apps, has been released, those apps will need domains. For that reason, the deadline of the .pi Domains Auction was extended to September 30, 2025, providing Pioneers with more time to innovate, build functioning apps, and bid on the .pi Domains they want for their apps.

The Pi2Day interactive Ecosystem Challenge

To celebrate the updates supporting Pi2Day's various systems and usefulness, Pioneers can take part in the Pi2Day Ecosystem Challenge. This challenge guides Pioneers to various new features, including many introduced in this announcement, and helps them explore the Pi ecosystem and Pi products. After completing steps that have a set order, Pioneers also receive fun digital prizes.

People are encouraged to take part as early as possible. The Ecosystem Challenge is held from June 27 to July 7.
