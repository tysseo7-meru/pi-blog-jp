Grace Period Deadline Extended to February 28, 2025

Published: February 28, 2025

Categories: announcement

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

As part of the continuing effort to ensure inclusiveness and fairness across Pi Network, the deadline of the Grace Period (the period with a deadline to finish KYC (identity verification) and Mainnet (live network) migration) is extended from January 31, 2025 to February 28, 2025. This change gives Pioneers additional time to finish KYC verification and Mainnet migration, helping more members protect their Pi and contribute to the growing ecosystem (the whole set of related apps and services).

Note that the Grace Period is independent of the Open Network timing. The Open Network may start before or after the Grace Period ends, and the Grace Period rules stay the same in either case.

Because the Grace Period deadline is sensitive and the consequences are serious, by extending the deadline to February 28, 2025, Pi wants to be as generous as possible so that more Pioneers have the chance to protect their Pi.

This decision reflects a commitment to making Pi Network accessible and fair for everyone. It also fits with, and pushes forward, the wider mission of preparing for the Open Network in the first quarter of 2025.

What this extension means

  • By February 28, 2025, submit your KYC application and finish the Mainnet Checklist to migrate to Mainnet
  • The Grace Period rules do not change. If you do not finish KYC or Mainnet migration within the Grace Period, you risk losing most of your mobile balance (except Pi mined in the most recent six months before migration)

Why the Grace Period is being extended

This extra month is designed to support Pioneers who still need time for the KYC and migration steps. It also reflects the commitment to inclusiveness and accessibility across the global Pi community. Like the previous extensions, this extension helps in the following ways.

  • Inclusiveness and fairness: it lets as many Pioneers as possible finish KYC and Mainnet migration, and minimizes the loss of Pi from missing the deadline
  • Further relief for specific cases: this extension once again serves as overall relief for people in specific cases. It gives more time overall to finish KYC and Mainnet migration, without spending excessive resources and time adjusting timers to solve cases one by one
  • Preparation for the Open Network: by having more Pioneers finish KYC and migration, the whole network becomes stronger before the Open Network launch. With more verified participants on Mainnet, a healthier and safer ecosystem is secured for the Open Network

Answers to expected questions

Some Pioneers may have questions about what this extension means for Pi Network as a whole, so this is clarified.

  • Does this extension affect the Open Network launch? No. This extension does not delay the Open Network launch plan and does not affect it. The Open Network remains on track for the first quarter of 2025. The Grace Period and the Open Network timing are independent
  • What happens if I miss the new Grace Period deadline? If you miss the February 28, 2025 deadline, only the Pi mined in the six months right before migration will be kept. Older balances outside that most recent period will be lost. Because multiple extensions have been provided, you are strongly urged to act now to protect your whole Pi balance
  • How much extra time was added to the Grace Period in total? A total of two extra months was added to the Grace Period, which was originally scheduled to end on December 31, 2024. According to the Whitepaper (the project plan document), the Grace Period was intended as a six-month period for Pioneers to finish KYC and Mainnet migration, but it has now been extended by two more months

What to do now

All Pioneers are strongly urged to finish the KYC application and Mainnet migration steps as soon as possible.

To finish the Grace Period requirements, first go to the Pi mining app and tap 'Mainnet Checklist' at the top of the screen to go to the Mainnet Checklist. Or, tap the hamburger icon at the top left to open the menu, then tap 'Mainnet', then tap 'Mainnet Checklist'.

All the steps to finish KYC and Mainnet migration are written in this Mainnet Checklist.

Note that Pioneers in a tentative KYC state, or Pioneers waiting for full KYC results and migration, may need to finish actions. For example, some Pioneers need to finish additional liveness checks (checks that confirm the person is present in person) — make sure the video is clear and in bright lighting. If the mining app asks you for such an additional liveness check, take the check and do not close the pop-up. Even if you are still waiting for KYC results, sign the token acknowledgment in the Mainnet Checklist, because everyone needs this eventually to migrate to Mainnet. Other Pioneers must secure their accounts in the way the Mainnet Checklist requires. By checking the KYC app and the Mainnet Checklist today and acting when guided, you can receive your final KYC results and be ready to migrate as soon as possible.

You also need to encourage the members of your referral team and security circle to meet their own deadlines.

Why the Grace Period matters

The Grace Period is essential to maintaining a real, human, decentralized ecosystem, and to ensuring its integrity and ability to grow in scale as Pi Network moves to the Open Network. The reasons the Grace Period matters are as follows.

  • Identity verification through KYC and migration: only Pioneers who finish KYC and the Mainnet Checklist can move their mined, transferable balance to the blockchain. This ensures that Pi in the Open Network is safe, free of bots (automatic programs), and rooted in real human participation. By putting verified users first, the Grace Period protects the network's long-term health, utility, and stability
  • Encouraging progress while giving time to migrate: the Grace Period balances giving Pioneers enough time to pass KYC and migrate with keeping enough urgency. This policy helps speed up progress toward the Open Network and its necessary preconditions. It also helps Pioneers move more of their referral and security circle bonuses to Mainnet, as a result of more members of referral teams and security circles passing KYC and migrating
  • Supporting the network's utility and growth: without KYC and migration, Pioneers cannot use Pi for payments in apps or for other transactions in the utility-based ecosystem. The Grace Period ensures timely participation, so that more Pioneers can engage with, actively contribute to, and benefit from the Pi ecosystem's growing utility
  • Preventing unverified Pi from affecting the network: this policy helps prevent unverified Pi beyond the most recent six-month period from migrating to Mainnet, and helps avoid creating excessive uncertainty, which is not good for the Open Network and the network plan. Instead, such Pi will be released in the future for mining by other KYC-verified Pioneers

In closing

The additional time provided by this extension is one more step so that more Pioneers can have the opportunity to fully take part in the Pi Network ecosystem. Whether you joined Pi at the very beginning or joined recently, this extension is designed with those people in mind. But you must not wait: act now to protect your Pi and become a member of the growing Mainnet community.

Together, we are building something truly innovative. As one united network, let us go through these final steps together to prepare for the Open Network.
