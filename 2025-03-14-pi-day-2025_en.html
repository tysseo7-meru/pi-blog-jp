Pi Day 2025: Expanding The Ecosystem With Utilities On All Levels After Open Network

Published: March 14, 2025

Categories: announcement, pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

With the start of the Open Network (the main live network that is connected to the outside world) and with outside connections turned on, Pi Network has entered a new period. It is a period that connects an active ecosystem (all the related apps and services together) to the wider blockchain world and expands real-world uses.

Millions of Pioneers (members of Pi Network) use Pi, tens of thousands of businesses have added Pi to their services, and many apps have been built. These apps meet the needs of the community and the ecosystem and are actually used. This shows the strength and stability of Pi not only to the Pi community, but also to the wider field of crypto assets and to the internet.

Pi Day 2025 celebrates the growth of the Pi ecosystem at all levels, from local trade to apps and to systems for the whole platform. The base for this is the strength and the combined resources of the global Pi community. For the past 6 years, Pioneers have grown the network, helped build its base, and worked to bring Pi into trade and to create uses for it. That work is now starting to bring results.

To support the growth of the ecosystem and the overall use of Pi, Pi released several features on this day. The global PiFest event also started. The items released are as follows.

  • .pi domain auction
  • Start of the PiFest shopping period
  • Wider applications to the Mainnet ecosystem
  • Improvement of the Mainnet ecosystem screen

.pi Domain Auction: A New Use for the Whole Platform

A domain (a name that works like an address on the internet) is an important base for any online ecosystem. On the first Pi Day after the Open Network, the growth of the ecosystem starts by letting participants and contributors secure their own .pi domain for their business, brand, store, content, app, website, personal use, and other uses. At the same time, through the .pi domain auction, Pi gives the Pi cryptocurrency a use for the whole platform. This system uses the combined resources of the Pi community and the outside connections made possible by the Open Network.

What Is a .pi Domain?

Traditional internet domains work as unique identifiers and digital addresses. They help individuals and businesses create an online presence that is easy to understand and easy to find, and they make movement, branding, and contact with users easier inside the global internet system. In the same way, .pi domains work as unique digital identifiers, or virtual storefronts, inside the Pi ecosystem. They make it easy to interact with websites, apps, and services that use Pi.

Overall, the running and organization of .pi domains mostly follow the system that has worked in the traditional internet domain industry for decades, including ownership, transfers, and yearly registration fees. However, .pi domains differ in two large ways. First, they work inside a Web3 environment (a decentralized web based on blockchain). They work directly with the Pi Browser, and also with third-party wallets and services that connect to this feature. They can also be used through the pinet.com extension that works in traditional browsers such as Chrome and Safari. Second, the process to get a .pi domain, including the auction and bidding, is carried out using blockchain technology (a shared record system that many computers keep together), and Pi is the only currency that can be used for payment. By using the Pi blockchain, openness, safety, and a direct use for the Pi cryptocurrency are secured.

How .pi Domains Differ from Many Other Virtual Properties

The main difference is that the concrete value of .pi domains is supported by the attention and involvement of more than 60 million Pioneers worldwide, more than 19 million identity-verified individuals, more than 12 million people on the blockchain, and people outside who are ready to trade through the blockchain and third-party services. In addition, tens of thousands of merchants worldwide accept Pi. The participation, involvement, and strength of the community were shown to the world after the start of the Open Network.

Another important difference is that .pi domains already have clear, ready-to-use, and measurable real-world uses and practical uses. Merchants can easily create a storefront that Pioneers can find easily, using a clear brand name domain. E-commerce apps (apps for buying and selling online) can quickly open online stores or blogs, and this can greatly increase community interaction and trade. Developers can build and publish custom apps on their own .pi domain, and this encourages new ideas and growth. Brands gain the benefits of a clear identity inside the ecosystem, a simpler start for users, and better chances to be found. All of this is available now inside the ecosystem. This is different from buying a virtual property in an empty Web3 social network with no one inside. In that case, the use of the property has not been realized, and its use is uncertain now and in the future.

How the .pi Domain Auction Creates a Use for Pi for the Whole Platform

A use for the whole platform means turning the combined resources of Pi into a use for the whole network. This use supports Pi as a currency for all Pioneers. It is different from a use inside one app, which is closed inside the setting and functions of a specific Pi app. The platform-wide use released most recently before this was the Pi Ad Network.

In the same way, the .pi domain auction is also a use for the whole platform. This is because it directly uses the combined resources of the Pi community that make .pi domain names usable, namely attention, being real, and a large community that is ready to trade.

The .pi auction is run only with the Pi cryptocurrency. People need to use Pi to bid for and buy something that has real meaning and purpose. No other currency or cryptocurrency is accepted or involved. All Pi received from the sale of .pi domains will be used for the Pi ecosystem, developer programs, events, and the promotion of uses for Pi.

The running and organization of the auction depend heavily on the Pi Mainnet blockchain (the main live blockchain) and increase its use. It also uses special features, such as claimable balances for Pioneers (a balance where the person can confirm receipt later) and multiplexed addresses (special shared addresses) for users of centralized exchanges (CEXs) (trading services run by a company) who hold Pi but are not yet Pioneers.

Rules for Taking Part in the Auction

The first .pi domain auction will be held as follows.

  • Who can take part: The whole Pi community and all individuals who can get Pi through a centralized exchange (CEX)
  • Period: The auction starts on Pi Day, March 14, 2025, and ends on Pi2Day, June 28, 2025. An individual auction for a specific domain name will be extended if there is a new bid within 24 hours before the end of that auction. In other words, an individual auction for a specific domain name ends only if there was no new bid in the 24 hours before its end
  • Rules and how to take part: Pioneers can take part by visiting https://domains.pinet.com in the Pi Browser, and they will be guided to related products such as the Pi Wallet to complete a bid
  • In the auction screen, enter the domain you want to secure for yourself
  • Bid for that domain (the minimum bid is 10 Pi)
  • The highest bidder wins and buys the .pi domain for exclusive use. However, as with traditional internet domain fees, there may be a yearly registration fee and maintenance fee in the future
  • How to bid: Pioneers can use the full functions of the Pi blockchain. With the claimable balance feature (a feature that lets a person confirm receipt later), Pi can be sent as a bid for a domain in a form that can be cancelled. This means that at any time before the end of the auction, a person can withdraw the amount they bid before. Soon after the start of the auction, people who hold Pi only through an exchange will also be able to take part in the auction. Exchanges do not support claimable balances, so the steps are different. In that case, the auction screen creates a dedicated Pi blockchain address for each domain the person wants to bid on (these special addresses start with the letter “M”). The person can then send Pi to those addresses to bid. Because these bids are carried out as normal payments, refunds are processed only after the end of the auction. For the transfer of a domain to these winners, they need to create a normal Pi blockchain account to receive their .pi domain. People who want to use the Pi blockchain can become Pioneers through the normal sign-up steps in the Pi mobile app

Note: The company keeps the right, at its sole discretion (its own decision alone), to cancel or remove a domain auction or a bid for any reason. For example, it may exclude from the auction domains that are reserved for official use or future use, and certain domains, such as pi, pinetwork, coreteam, and maps. Vulgar or offensive words will also be excluded. Participants in a cancelled auction will get back the exact amount they put in as a bid, at any time through the claimable balance feature, or, if they are not Pioneers, after June 28, 2025. A gas fee applies (a fee for using the blockchain). Auction participants need to pay a gas fee for each bid added (0.01 Pi), changed (0.02 Pi), and cancelled (0.01 Pi). In practice, an additional or higher gas fee may apply, as decided by the blockchain. The company keeps the right, at its sole and absolute discretion, to change or revise these rules at any time, with or without notice to participants.

First PiFest in the Open Network: The Shopping Period Begins

The shopping period for the first PiFest in the Open Network has officially started, and it runs from March 14, 2025 to Friday, March 21, 2025. With the full support of Open Network connections, people can visit local businesses that accept Pi and shop with Pi. People can find local businesses through the Map of Pi app and take an active part through the full Pi ecosystem for bringing local trade together. The merchant registration period started 2 days ago. At present, registered sellers are more than 100,000, and 49,000 of them are active sellers on Map of Pi.

People can share their experiences on Fireside Forum (the #PiFest channel) and on social media. People can use the Map of Pi app to find participating merchants nearby, and use the Pi Wallet to easily pay and receive payments on the Pi Mainnet blockchain. Posts to Fireside Forum may also be introduced on Pi social media accounts. Merchants must also not forget to promote their own businesses.

PiFest supports uses in local trade and shows the real-world use of Pi around the world. Details of the guidelines for the first PiFest in the Open Network can be checked at the linked page.

Applications to the Mainnet Ecosystem Are Expanded

So that developers who want to build Pi apps can start easily, the application to start building, or to publish a Pi app that has already been built, is designed to be simple and clear and easy for developers to use. However, the standard to list a Pi app on the Pi ecosystem screen in the Pi Browser is much higher. It includes app quality, how complete the functions are, an evaluation of the use, and following stricter Pi ecosystem policies and guidelines. The reason for this high standard is that a Pi app needs to go above a minimum level before it can benefit from access by Pioneers inside the ecosystem, and at the same time it also gives developers a reason to build better apps that follow the policies and meet the needs of Pioneers.

Even with the high standard for ecosystem listing described above, this process has been improved to make applying even simpler and easier to use. As a result, developers who meet the requirements to publish a Mainnet app can now go directly to the Developer Portal and apply to add their app to the Mainnet ecosystem screen, without waiting to join a whitelist (a list of people approved in advance).

Before, an app needed to be given eligibility before it could apply to be listed on the Pi ecosystem screen in the Pi Browser. Now, this pre-approval step (approval before applying) has been removed. In other words, eligible developers no longer need to wait for approval in order to apply. With this change, the process is more efficient, and even more developers will be able to apply to have their apps included on the Mainnet ecosystem screen.

This update is important for the following reasons.

  • Many developers who meet the Mainnet requirements will be able to apply without delay
  • The number of Mainnet apps that can be seen inside the Pi ecosystem will increase
  • By expanding the number and quality of Mainnet apps, it supports the uses and growth of the network

Pi already has a group of published Mainnet Pi apps, and this forms the base for adding and publishing even more Pi apps on the Mainnet ecosystem screen. The list of Mainnet apps does not include all Mainnet apps and Mainnet-ready apps. This is because selection is limited based on quality, the use provided, and following Pi ecosystem policies and guidelines. Some third-party Pi apps that act as a guide to Pi Mainnet apps show more, or all, Mainnet apps, so Pioneers can refer to them for a wider overall view of the apps that can be used.

Note: Applying does not guarantee that an app will be included. To be approved, an app still needs to meet the requirements for listing in the Mainnet ecosystem.

Improved Design of the Ecosystem Screen

The design of the ecosystem screen has been improved in its organization, so Pioneers can move through community apps in a more natural and effective way, and the user experience is improved with a cleaner design. Before, each app had a label such as “Mainnet App” or “Not on PiNet”, and the screen was cluttered. As part of the recent update, those labels have been replaced with an “M” badge for Mainnet apps, a “T” badge for Testnet apps (apps on the test network), and a “!” badge for apps that are not on PiNet. When a Pioneer taps an app to see more details, an explanation of these badges is also shown inside the app page.
