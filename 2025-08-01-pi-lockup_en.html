Lockup Reminder: Pioneers Can Choose to Lock Up Pi to Boost Mining Rate!

Published: August 1, 2025

Categories: general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Regardless of their migration status, Pioneers can create a lockup by their own choice. A pre-migration lockup setting boosts mining on their current Pi based on a future lockup setting, and affects the Pi of the next migration. A post-migration lockup applies to the Pi a Pioneer has obtained on the Mainnet (production network) blockchain, and the Pioneer can freely lock it up.

To set a pre-migration lockup, tap the ☰ hamburger icon in the Pi mining app, and tap "Mainnet". Then tap "Configure Lockup Rate".

To set a post-migration lockup, tap the "Lockups" button in the Pi Wallet inside Pi Browser. At that time, you are to select the Mainnet wallet.

The mining boost becomes effective from the next mining session after the lockup.

When you create a new lockup, your individual mining rate goes up, based on the lockup reward system described in detail in the 2021 whitepaper. This helps support a strong and stable ecosystem and encourages long-term engagement with the network.

Differences between post-migration and pre-migration lockups

Both types of lockup follow the same reward calculation, and once decided, they cannot be cancelled. However, the Pi they apply to and the way they are carried out differ. As stated earlier, a pre-migration lockup setting boosts mining on the current Pi based on a future lockup setting, and affects the next migration's Pi. A post-migration lockup setting, on the other hand, applies to Pi that has already been obtained on the Mainnet blockchain and can be freely locked up. As for how they are carried out: a pre-migration lockup is applied at the time the migratable balance is migrated, while the post-migration lockup system locks up the Pioneer's Pi directly on the blockchain, right after the confirmation on the wallet screen.

Another difference is that the 200% lockup, which uses 200% of the amount already migrated, becomes available with post-migration lockups. Many Pioneers who have migrated to Mainnet may obtain additional Pi through other means besides mining, for example through Pi apps or local commerce. For that reason, a 200% lockup is possible, and Pioneers can use this feature to maximize their lockup mining rewards.
