100 Days of Open Network: A Utility-Driven Ecosystem

Published: June 19, 2025

Categories: general, update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

More than 100 days have passed since Open Network started. Open Network (the live network that can connect to outside systems) was the result of more than 6 years of building Pi Network's base and systems. During those years, Pi made sure that its uses were ready to use at the time of launch.

With the external connections of Open Network, Pi's active system, new apps, and wide network of people can now work with the wider blockchain world. Because of this, businesses can also connect with Pi after they pass the business check process of KYB (a check that confirms a business).

Open Network was important for Pi's mission. That mission is to create a cryptocurrency that anyone can join, that is centered on real use, that is widely adopted, and that anyone can access. Pioneers (Pi users) around the world are the driving force of Pi. They include people who know a lot about crypto, ordinary people in general, and everyone in between. This is because Pi aims to solve the problems of wide adoption by the public and real use.

In the first 100 days of Open Network, Pi's systems and uses kept bringing benefits to Pioneers and kept growing. Here, the article looks back at the progress and growth of the network, including important updates about use.

Pi Network Ventures: A $100 Million Investment in Innovative Startups

Pi Network Ventures is a $100 million development effort started by the Pi Foundation. It is held in Pi and US dollars. It invests in a planned way in startups and businesses. This includes businesses that move forward the use, adoption, and real-world impact of Pi.

Its main goals are three: to increase the use of Pi, to make it possible for more of the world's production to happen on Pi Network, and to bring Pi into the real world.

By investing in startups and businesses, Pi is trying to give power to developers. Many of them build with Pi, connect with Pi, create new ideas with Pi, and bring real use. Pi Network Ventures investments look for creators who focus on projects that can be reached around the world, that are led by the community, and that have real economic meaning.

Games are an important area that Pi Network Ventures focuses on for investment. Games sit where several important strengths meet: social involvement, the attention economy (an economy built on people's attention), and virtual goods. Pi Network Ventures is in a special position to use Pi Network to help game developers reach their goals. That, in turn, brings benefits to Pi's system.

For this reason, Pi Network started FruityPi. It is a fun fruit-matching game. It also shows Pi's existing game uses working in real life. By connecting with different Pi products and uses, such as the Pi Ad Network, Pi Wallet, and Pi payments, FruityPi has been used by Pi Network as an early sign. It shows that games, with their natural social movement and virtual economies, can move forward both involvement at the app level and wider use of the whole system at the same time.

PiFest and the Growth of Local Commerce with Pi

Pi Day 2025 was the start of the first PiFest after Open Network. It was a one-week event held from March 14, 2025 to March 21, 2025. With the external connections of Open Network working, PiFest supported commerce connected with Pi. It gave bigger chances for business use and adoption, and for Pioneer involvement. This included smooth deals on the Pi Mainnet (the live network) blockchain and deals through related Pi products. At PiFest, the following involvement was seen.

  • The total number of registered sellers was over 125,000, and 58,000 of them were active sellers
  • Over 1.8 million Pioneers used the Map of Pi app, which was made by the community
  • Over 45,000 reviews were sent in by Pioneers

The success of PiFest shows that Pioneers and businesses are eager to be involved with Pi Network and to do deals in Pi. It confirms that Pi Network's long-term view is being reached and will keep growing. That view is to build a system of people, open to everyone and powered by Pi.

.pi Domains Auction: A New Basic-Level Use

On Pi Day 2025, the .pi Domains Auction was also introduced. This is a basic-level use. It uses the shared resources of the Pi community — attention, being real, and a large community that is ready to do deals — together with the external connections of Open Network. It further grows Pi's role in real commerce and digital involvement.

With .pi Domains, individuals, apps, and businesses can bid for and own their own digital identifier (a name that identifies them online) inside Pi's system. .pi Domains already have clear, immediate, and measurable real uses, and practical uses now. This is what separates .pi Domains from virtual property and ownership in other systems that have developed more slowly.

So far, the .pi Domains Auction has had the following results.

  • Over 123,000 active bids
  • Over 57,000 separate bidders
  • Over 69,000 domains with active bids
  • The active bids during the auction period were made up of over 3 million Pi in total

Ecosystem Interface Updates

The Ecosystem Interface lists Mainnet apps that meet the conditions and that Pioneers can use. It keeps following Pi's mission. It gives power to Pioneers and developers by giving access to apps and uses, and by giving developers more chances to show their apps. In the first 100 days of Open Network, Pi Network made several updates to the Ecosystem Interface. The updates include the following.

  • Growth of Mainnet system apps: Developers who meet the requirements to release a Mainnet app can now apply directly through the Developer Portal to add their app to the Mainnet Ecosystem Interface, without waiting to be put on a whitelist (a list of approved people or apps)
  • Better design of the Ecosystem Interface: The design of the Ecosystem Interface was updated, with a cleaner design and a better user experience, so that Pioneers can find community apps in a natural way
  • Growth of Pi Ad Network applications from Pi apps: Pi app developers can now apply to join the Pi Ad Network. Chosen developers will join the first group of Pi apps in the test effort. Through this effort, they are now earning and receiving ad income
  • New apps join the Mainnet Ecosystem Interface: Since Open Network, new Pi apps have been added to the Mainnet Ecosystem Interface. This shows ongoing support for Pi Network from the community, users, and developers, and the ongoing growth of apps across Pi's whole system in the first 100 days after Open Network

Network Metrics

The current main figures that show the growth and size of the network are as follows.

  • Since Open Network, over 3 million more Pioneers have migrated to Mainnet, for a total of over 13 million Mainnet users
  • Over 7.4 billion Pi has been migrated. 5.2 billion Pi is locked, and 2.2 billion Pi is unlocked
  • Across the whole Pi blockchain (Testnet1, Testnet2, and Mainnet), over 400,000 Nodes (computers that support the network) are active

Pioneers can check the Pi Blockexplorer to see the latest figures. They can also read more about Pi's tokenomics (the economic design of the token) and supply at the guided place.

Account Security, Mainnet Migration, and Mainnet Wallet Activation

Since Open Network, Pi has kept supporting Pioneers through updates about account safety and Mainnet migration. It also widened ease of access to Mainnet through Mainnet wallet activation.

  • Full phone number change for Pioneers who finished KYC: Pioneers who passed KYC (an identity check) can now update their full phone number after completing a liveness check test (a test that confirms the person is really the person)
  • Two-factor authentication for Pi migration wallets, to make sure Pioneer accounts are safe (2FA, a system that adds one more step of checking on top of the usual check): Pioneers must complete 2FA to keep their migration Pi Wallet safe and to confirm it. For Pioneers affected by this requirement, it must be completed before their Pi is successfully migrated to the Mainnet blockchain
  • Move to email-based 2FA: With this safety measure, checking through a trusted email becomes closer, safer, and better in cost. Email-based 2FA does not have the limits of SMS-based checking. Those limits include limits by place due to communication limits, phone company policies, personal service contracts, and costs
  • Wider ease of access to the Pi Mainnet system through new wallet activation chances: With the Mainnet wallet activation feature, verified Pioneers — including people who have fully finished KYC, most people with tentative KYC, and users who have not yet joined Pi Network — can activate a wallet on the Pi Mainnet blockchain. This removes barriers to taking part in the Pi Mainnet system and widens inclusion
  • A new account recovery option is now available: A new flow for password reset was introduced. Pioneers who set a trusted email for their account can use it. If they set one, they can ask for a password reset and receive a password reset link at their trusted email address. This is in addition to the existing recovery methods already available to Pioneers

Conclusion

Pi's system on Open Network is defined by efforts for apps and uses, by real and practical ways to use Pi, and by involvement from the Pi community. In the first 100 days of Open Network, Pioneers showed that a Pi cryptocurrency that is widely adopted and based on real use is here to stay and will grow. The conclusion says that Pioneers, developers, and businesses should keep being involved and keep building on Pi. They should push the network forward and keep reaching results that benefit the whole system.
