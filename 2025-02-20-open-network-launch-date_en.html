Pi’s Open Network Set to Launch February 20, 2025!

Published: February 20, 2025

Categories: announcement, update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

To all Pioneers: Pi Network will officially transition to the Open Network (the period connected to the outside) of the Mainnet (the live network) at 8:00 a.m. UTC on February 20, 2025.

As a result of the efforts and work of the whole Pi community over the past six years, the Pi community is taking a big next step toward realizing Pi's vision: the world's most inclusive peer-to-peer ecosystem (the whole set of related apps and services) and online experience, powered by Pi, Pi Network's native currency.

With the improvements announced in the recent period update, Mainnet migrations reached 10.14 million, passing the original goal of 10 million. Pi is ready to open a utility-driven ecosystem, and more than 19 million identity-verified Pioneers can now use Pi, a cryptocurrency backed by real functions and uses.

On the launch day, you can watch for more information about the Open Network. For now, this article looks at Pi's journey, what to expect when the Open Network launches, updates on the Open Network conditions, and how you can prepare now.

The journey so far

Pi Network's journey of more than six years toward the Open Network has been built on milestones and deliberate stages driven forward by Pioneers, the ecosystem, and the community. The current third phase of the Mainnet began in December 2021, with the start of the Enclosed Period (the period not connected to the outside). This meant that the Mainnet was live, but had a firewall (a system that blocks connections from outside) to prevent outside connections. This period set the stage for the Open Network and gave time for the following.

  • Pioneers to complete KYC (identity check) and obtain Pi on the Mainnet
  • Developers to build real apps and use cases for the Pi ecosystem
  • The Core Team to release and improve various Pi features and use cases

A new mining reward issuance formula was also released early in the Enclosed Period, in March 2022. It follows a declining exponential model, and it sizes Pioneers' rewards for their contributions to the network appropriately, while balancing the needs of network growth, accessibility, longevity, and scarcity.

The Grace Period was put in place on July 1, 2024, and it sped up progress toward the Open Network. This was to balance giving Pioneers enough time to pass KYC and qualify to obtain Pi on the Mainnet, and creating enough motivation and urgency for people to pass KYC and move to the Open Network. By preventing unverified Pi older than the most recent six-month KYC period from obtaining Pi on the Mainnet, this strategy helps Pi be released for mining by other Pioneers.

Throughout that time, Pioneers who obtained Pi on the Mainnet could trade Pi with other Pioneers inside the network. For example, PiFest 2024 was a one-week event. At a local level, it encouraged Pi transactions for goods and services between Pi merchants and Pioneer shoppers. Across 160 countries, it attracted more than 27,000 active sellers and 28,000 test merchants, and more than 950,000 Pioneers took part in just a few days. PiFest showed Pi's complete ecosystem for local commerce integration — including finding shops through the Map of Pi, paying with the Pi Wallet, transactions on the Pi Mainnet blockchain, and social sharing on Fireside Forum — all working together smoothly across different existing Pi apps.

What to expect from the Open Network at launch

The launch of the Open Network brings an important new change — external connectivity — to the first-layer Pi blockchain. This creates an environment where Pi can connect safely with outside systems, and widens opportunities for Pioneers and businesses.

Connecting Pi with the outside world

With the move to the Open Network, external connectivity becomes possible on the Mainnet blockchain, and Pi can work with other compliant networks and systems. This means Pioneers can also make transactions outside the Pi ecosystem, widening Pi's utility and reach.

At the point when the Open Network launches and the firewall is removed, technically anyone can add a Node (a computer that supports the network) to the Mainnet blockchain, in the sense of running the protocol (the rules for communication) and connecting to the network. The Core Team will gradually invite Pioneers from the Testnet to the Mainnet through the desktop Node screen, giving priority to people with strong past contributions and reliability scores. Node ranking information will be published as soon as possible.

Compliance requirements for businesses to join the network

To keep a safe and compliant ecosystem, taking part in activities on the Mainnet blockchain requires a KYC check (Know Your Customer, identity check) for Pioneers, and a KYB check (Know Your Business, business verification) for businesses. Overall, Pi intends to be a safe Web3 space (a decentralized web based on blockchains), where Pioneers can connect with the outside using the Pi they hold, and where KYB-verified businesses and partners can work with real, KYC-verified Pioneers — and the reverse is also true.

Businesses can apply for KYB verification through the linked web page. When the Open Network launches, Pioneers will be able to see a list of KYB-verified businesses on Pi's website.

Update on the Open Network conditions

The Open Network date was chosen based on the Open Network conditions, which were set in December 2023 and shared with the whole community. Pi Network's progress toward finishing the Open Network conditions has continued and sped up through the community's efforts since the December 2024 period update. The network has now reached all the conditions needed for the Open Network, so the time for the Open Network has come.

The conditions are looked at in detail below.

  • Condition 1: All the technical, product, business, and legal work needed to reach the Open Network is finished
  • Condition 2: 19 million Pioneers verified their identity through the Pi KYC system, including full KYC and Tentative KYC, as originally defined in the conditions. This passes the conditions' original requirement of 15 million people. 10.14 million Pioneers migrated to the Mainnet, passing the original goal of 10 million. The network reached more than 100 Mainnet apps or Mainnet-ready apps, meeting the goal of 100 apps: providing the active, engaged, and diverse utility that Pi Network needs to thrive in the Open Network at launch
  • Condition 3: The Open Network launch will not be delayed further because of external factors or an unfavorable environment

Pioneers should continue KYC and migration to the Mainnet, if they have not finished them yet, whether before or after the Open Network launch, and engage with Pi apps in the Pi Browser, to support the ecosystem's development and long-term utility. In the same way, community developers are encouraged to develop, refine, and release apps that meet Pi Network's standards and contribute to the community and its utility.

What Pioneers and developers should do during the Open Network period

Contributions from Pioneers and community developers are essential to the network's strength and resilience as new challenges arise in the Open Network phase.

The ways Pioneers can take an active part are as follows.

  • Mining and contributions: keep mining, and the rate is adjusted in line with the declining exponential issuance model and the monthly cap
  • Building utility: engage with Pi apps, and support Pi Network and Pi through use of the platform and Pi, helping to widen the ecosystem's utility. Developers are encouraged to refine existing solutions and create new solutions that meet the real needs of the Pi community and beyond
  • Local commerce integration: shop owners are encouraged to keep accepting Pi, so that Pioneers can take part in peer-to-peer transactions for goods and services, bringing ongoing utility to Pi in Pioneers' daily lives
  • KYC and migration: the network should continue the KYC and migration process after the Open Network launch as well. If you have not finished yet, you need to finish as soon as possible during the Open Network period

For developers, there is the following.

  • Improve Mainnet apps: make sure to polish apps, to meet users' needs and requests and to deal with problems that arise after the Mainnet
  • Develop new Mainnet apps: build Pi apps and contribute to the Pi ecosystem. Details can be checked at the linked page

As Pi Network enters the Open Network period, Pioneers, developers, and businesses are invited to work together actively, drive innovation forward, and make sure Pi Network remains a sustainable, decentralized, and solid community.
