Vibe Coding Meets Pi App Studio: Build AI-Created Apps for 60M+ People

Published: May 13, 2026

Categories: announcement, update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

People who do vibe coding (a way of building apps by giving instructions to AI) and other creators can now bring AI-created apps into Pi's real distribution network and utility ecosystem through Pi App Studio. Through this, they can reach more than 60 million Engaged Pioneers (Pi users who are actively involved).

Creators can be technical developers or non-technical product people. They build apps with AI-assisted coding tools such as Codex, Claude Code, Replit, Cursor, and Lovable, or other similar tools, and afterwards they can use Pi App Studio to convert an app built outside into a Pi app. The new option provides a dedicated copy-and-paste prompt (a written instruction for the AI). Creators use it in the external AI tool to bring in the Pi SDK (a development kit for adding Pi's features to an app), check the settings, and add payments in Pi. The new flow makes it easier for creators who already use AI tools to bring their apps, services, and products into the Pi ecosystem through a simple integration process.

This update is in line with Pi's wider AI efforts: efforts to expand the ways AI and Pi are used together to build apps, services, and online businesses. Pi App Studio already helps Pioneers build apps using AI and Pi within the Pi ecosystem. With this new feature, App Studio also extends its support to creators who are not yet part of the Pi community but who build apps with various external AI tools and are looking for a place to distribute them. This widens the layer of creators doing vibe coding on Pi.

The idea behind this effort is as follows. As AI greatly lowers the barrier to building software such as apps, tools, and online services, competition over distribution will grow more intense, and Pi Network can provide the solution to that. Creators, on their side, help further expand the Pi ecosystem and Pi's utility.

The original article includes a video showing how the flow actually works. The example used is a simple Connect 4 game app built on an external AI coding platform. The video shows Pi App Studio bringing the app into the Pi ecosystem, without the creator having to rebuild the integration work by hand from zero. The dedicated copy-and-paste AI prompt was developed on various external AI platforms and tested carefully and repeatedly. The purpose is to make sure the app integrates the Pi SDK correctly and quickly, and also follows Pi Network's developer guidelines. This method not only reduces development and integration time for creators doing vibe coding; because fit with the Pi ecosystem is already built in through the AI prompt, it may also shorten the process for the app to receive Mainnet (the live network) approval.

The whole integration into Pi through this method can be finished in as little as two minutes. However, it varies depending on the specific external AI platform and the size and complexity of the app's code.

As a note, access to payments on Mainnet is granted to apps that show real utility, safe behavior, stable operation, and legitimate use cases denominated in Pi.

How Pi helps AI-created apps gain users and grow

As AI lowers the barrier to building software, competition for human attention and user acquisition will grow more intense. More people can build apps, tools, and services. But building is only part of making a useful product. Creators also need a way to reach real users, payment infrastructure, and a community where the app is found and used.

Pi can play that role. It is a distribution network and utility ecosystem where AI-created apps can connect with Pioneers verified through KYC (identity verification procedures), through a relatively simple process.

Pi also provides Pi sign-in, Pi Wallet, identity verification, the SDK, infrastructure resources, the Pi Ad Network (Pi's advertising network), payment features, and decentralized human infrastructure. This reduces the need for creators to build these systems from zero.

For this reason, the new flow is especially relevant to creators who build apps through vibe coding. Vibe coding helps turn an idea into an app quickly. Pi App Studio, on the other hand, helps connect that app to a real ecosystem where it is used, tried, and improved.

In other words, a person who builds an app with AI can, just by connecting their service or product to Pi Network, have an online business with real users, built-in payments, and a living ecosystem. This is part of Pi's effort to provide an answer to what individual economic participation can look like in the AI era.

If you do vibe coding, or know someone building an AI app who needs a place to distribute it, you are guided to go to Pi App Studio today, convert your AI-created app into a Pi app, and get in touch with the Pi ecosystem's users, infrastructure, and monetization opportunities.

Pi App Studio is available from Pi Browser or the Pi Node desktop app.

Pioneers are also called on to share this blog online with relevant vibe coding communities, to support creators looking for a place to distribute their apps.
