Celebrate Pi2Day 2025 with the Ecosystem Challenge on Pi Network!

Published: July 7, 2025

Categories: pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The Ecosystem Challenge starts today and continues until July 7, 2025. Note that the publication date written in this article's text is June 28, 2025.

Pi2Day has arrived. As we celebrate another year of growth and progress, Pioneers are invited to take part in this year's Pi2Day Ecosystem Challenge. It is an interactive journey through the Pi ecosystem that you advance at your own pace, with fun rewards along the way.

This challenge offers Pioneers the opportunity to explore Pi App Studio and Ecosystem Directory Staking (a system that supports the ranking of apps within the ecosystem), as well as other parts of the ecosystem. With the Open Network (the production network that can connect to the outside), it is now a time when users can engage more deeply than ever with the apps, tools, and community that characterize Pi.

Whether you are a person who builds apps, an active community member, or someone who simply wants to explore, this challenge is for you.

As you complete the Ecosystem Challenge actions, a series of limited in-app rewards that show your achievement and show your contribution to the Pi ecosystem will be unlocked.

How to start

  • Open the Pi mining app and tap the Ecosystem Challenge button on the home screen
  • Follow the list of actions. In each action, you are prompted to explore, engage, or complete a meaningful step within the Pi ecosystem
  • Complete all the steps to unlock a special reward and celebrate Pi2Day together with the Pioneer community around the world

The article closes with the words "Happy Pi2Day!".
