.pi Domains Auction Update and Utility Strategy

Published: June 12, 2025

Categories: update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The .pi Domains Auction continues to see strong engagement across the whole Pi ecosystem.

As first shared in the Pi Day 2025 announcement and the Domains Auction FAQs, the Domains Auction is a platform-level utility. It uses the Pi community's collective resources and the external connections of the Open Network (the live network that can connect with the outside), and it further advances Pi's role in real commerce and digital engagement. Through it, Pioneers get the chance to secure and hold custom .pi domains for businesses, brands, stores, content, apps, websites, personal use, and more.

Recently, several updates were released, including a new statistics page available in the Domains Auction app.

However, as the auction continues, the article notes that certain trends and challenges need attention, so that .pi Domains succeed in the long term within the wider Pi ecosystem and deliver their intended utility.

Recent updates

Auction statistics now available

A dedicated screen now shows the main Domains Auction statistics in real time. Pioneers can see the domains with the most bids, the highest prices, recent bidding trends, and other information. This feature gives participants a transparent, current view of how the auction is progressing and where the most activity is happening.

The .pi Domains Auction became a Pi app

The .pi Domains Auction is now a Pi app. The auction was previously started through the Pi Wallet, but separating it makes it possible to release its own features, such as the statistics page and email notifications.

Other updates

The bidding screens have been polished overall, giving a cleaner and smoother experience.

Access to Mainnet (live network) wallets has become much easier, so more Pioneers can view and take part in the .pi Domains Auction.

An upcoming feature will also make email notifications possible. Through the Settings tab, Pioneers will be notified about updates on individual domains.

Creating utility and the .pi Domains Auction

As the auction moves forward, some new trends worth examining have appeared. A considerable share of the active bidding focuses on domain squatting (taking names in order to hold them). In other words, those bidders are trying to get domain names connected to major brands, common words, and popular phrases. This shifts the focus away from the most important goal, which is utility.

.pi Domains are designed as working Web3 identifiers — that is, as anchors for apps, services, and stores that run on Pi.

What is seen now is early momentum from this domain squatting based on owning names. However, the real value of a .pi domain comes from active use. For developers and businesses in the Pi ecosystem, these domains should serve as easy-to-recognize entrances to apps that actually work, such as a business's store, tools, blog, or something more advanced.

To fully support that original vision, the domain auction should develop alongside the rollout of tools that help Pioneers build apps, not only alongside bidding and sales.

Work on solutions to these possible problems, such as making app building more accessible than before, is in progress, and they will be announced soon.
