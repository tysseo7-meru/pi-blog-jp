Ecosystem Directory Staking Visual Update: Stake Pi to Support and Promote Pi Apps and Utilities in the Ecosystem

Published: June 18, 2026

Categories: developer, general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Ecosystem Directory Staking is a decentralized way for Pioneers (Pi users) and businesses to support and promote the ranking of Pi apps and utilities inside the Ecosystem Interface (the Pi app listing screen). Staking here means a system where you support an app by putting in Pi for a set period.

The Ecosystem Interface is where Pioneers can browse Pi apps by category in Pi Browser. It includes Mainnet apps, Pi App Studio apps, and services such as centralized exchanges.

Pioneers and businesses can directly lift the ranking of an app or service in the ecosystem by staking their own Pi on the Mainnet blockchain (a technology that manages transaction records in a distributed way). Staking increases an app's chance to be seen by the Pi community, which can lead to more views and more users coming in. This feature gives developers and creators a tool they can use themselves, using Pi to draw on Pi Network's resource of attention and gain users. The community can also evaluate apps together, not only through comments like in ordinary Web2 app listings, but through real stakes that carry economic weight.

Staking was originally launched in June 2025, and it is a lightweight, platform-level use of Pi. Compared with the Pi Ad Network and Pi Launchpad, it does not need much work in application and approval procedures, and the effort for developers to set up and join, in order to access Pi's attention and user-acquisition resources, is minimal.

For example, less than a week after CiDi Games launched its beta, the app's ranking was lifted naturally by 3.19 million Pi of staking. This led to strong exposure and more than 1.2 million game plays in under a week. This shows that Ecosystem Directory Staking serves as a visible signal of community interest.

This week, Ecosystem Directory Staking released an updated design, with various improvements to the user experience, preparing it for further use by developers, creators, vibe coders (people who build apps using AI), and Pioneers. App rankings can be seen in the PiApps tab of Pi Browser, and with this update, discovery and navigation in the ecosystem became easier.

Ecosystem Directory Staking is especially useful at a time when AI-assisted tools make apps easier to build, while reaching real users is becoming harder. It gives vibe coders, developers, and creators a way, using Pi, to have their apps discovered, supported, and improved by the more than 60 million engaged Pioneers in the Pi ecosystem.

As a note, there is no protocol-level (base-system level) Pi reward for staking. This is because the network does not promote specific Pi apps over other apps. Whether to provide incentives for staking — for example app features, overall quality, user experience, in-app rewards, promotions, or other benefits — is decided by the outside developers of each Pi app or service. The original staked amount is returned to the user after the staking period ends.
