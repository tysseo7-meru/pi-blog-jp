Upcoming Protocol v25 Upgrade and Mining App Design Updates

The upcoming protocol v25 upgrade: new privacy-protecting capabilities

Pi plans to upgrade to protocol v25 on Wednesday, July 22. This upgrade mainly focuses on improving the stability and reliability of the network. It also adds new capabilities for smart contracts (systems of agreements that run automatically on the blockchain) that are more efficient and protect privacy.

Protocol v25 introduces two technical components: BN254 cryptography and the Poseidon hash. With these, developers can build modern zero-knowledge applications (apps that use a method to prove that something is correct without showing the content itself). With this capability, an application can confirm that a claim or piece of information is valid while protecting the user's privacy. It supports uses such as identity verification and checking other proofs.

For example, a user can prove that they meet identity verification or participation requirements without sharing all of their underlying personal information with the app.

Before protocol v25, building this kind of application was much harder.

If you have not done so yet, you are asked to update your Node to v25 as soon as possible in order to stay connected to the network. There will also be follow-up news about the upgrade to v26. Details can be checked at the linked page.

Side menu and app profile screen updates: the first step in refreshing the mining app experience

Pi has updated the design of the Pi mining app's side menu and the app's profile screen. Important Pioneer information and ecosystem features are now easier to find, easier to understand, and easier to move through. This is the first step in a wider, staged renewal of the mining app and the home screen.

Feedback from Pioneers has been received. They said the mining app works well, but that the time has come for a new look and an improved experience. This update is Pi's response to that, and it is only the first step.

The Pi mining app is one of the most important screens (interfaces) in the Pi ecosystem. It has supported the growth of the community of more than 60 million engaged Pioneers who know what happens when they mine (earn) Pi every day. Because of this central role, changes to the design and the user experience must be introduced carefully, in stages, rather than all at once. The goal is to keep the core functions that have worked well, and to improve clarity, appearance, and ease of use while continuing to learn from Pioneers' feedback.

The renewed side menu shows important information and ecosystem components in a clearer, more organized form, with better UI (the look and operation of the screen) style and visual priority throughout. Pioneers can now more easily see items such as the Mainnet Checklist, mining information, featured Pi apps, and other important parts of the Pi experience, all in one place.

Dark mode (a screen display in dark colors), which many Pioneers had asked for, has also been added.

To see the update, tap the hamburger (☰) icon at the top left of the Pi mining app. As this renewed experience continues to develop, Pioneers' feedback will be used for future improvements.

Readers are asked what they think of the new design and what they would like to see next, and are shown where to post their feedback. That feedback will help shape the next steps of this important renewal.

Update to Mainnet wallet creation through Fast Track

Because Fast Track KYC (a system that moves identity verification along faster) can now be used to help Pioneers complete the identity verification needed to create a Mainnet wallet directly within the Pi app experience, Pi is removing Banxa as an in-app option for Mainnet wallet creation from the app's UI.

Banxa remains a KYB-verified (verified as a business) third-party service. In supported regions, Pi remains available through Banxa. Pioneers can continue to use Banxa as a third-party option in regions where it is available.
