.pi Domains Auction Concludes: Next Steps for Pioneers

Published: September 30, 2025

Categories: general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The .pi domains auction period ended on September 30, 2025. This is a milestone for this effort, which is a platform-level utility (a basic use that supports the whole system). Pioneers (Pi users) can expect an orderly process to claim domains that checks that domains are used for real purposes, including online apps and commerce (buying and selling), and lowers the risk of malicious hoarding (buying up many domains in a harmful way).

How the auction ended

All domain auctions officially ended on September 30. After this date, the following applies.

  • No new bids will be accepted.
  • However, if a bid was placed on a domain within the last 24 hours of the auction, the bidding period for that domain will automatically be extended by 24 hours.
  • If another bid is placed during the extended period, it will be extended by another 24 hours, and so on in the same way.

This system of extending step by step is as set out in the original auction rules, and it makes sure things are fair for all Pioneers who are actively taking part. When the auction for an individual domain ends, the winning Pioneer secures their bid for the domain they wanted.

How to claim a domain

After the auction ends, there will be a data review period of about two months. This stage is essential to check the auction results and prepare for the domain claiming process.

After the review period, eligible winners can start claiming their domains. To make sure .pi domains are used for good progress in the ecosystem and to prevent unfair hoarding at low cost that would stop future legitimate projects from having domains, the claiming process, at least in the first period, is as follows.

  • Domain winners need to connect a Pi app for that domain in order to claim the domain.
  • The connected Pi app needs to have reached a certain level of natural use. Details on how to reach this level will be provided after the review period ends. For example, for a shop use case, this process includes linking the shop's website URL, connecting the wallet (a place to hold and use Pi) used for transactions, and writing briefly how the domain supports the use.
  • Pioneers can complete the conditions above and claim their domain within up to one year from the start of the claiming process. This makes sure that winners who are real builders and businesses have time to complete the conditions and claim their domains.

Note that the eligibility criteria (the conditions for being able to claim a domain) may be loosened or changed in the future.

Through this effort, domains stay connected to real apps, shops, and uses in the ecosystem and remain available for them.

Renewal and safeguards

One year after the claiming feature is released, domains will need to be renewed. This is the same as traditional domain registration outside Pi. To prevent people from holding domains without using them, renewal will also require meeting criteria similar to those above. For example, an app will need to show real use and usage, and a shop may be required to meet a minimum number of wallet transactions.

Giving priority to real uses

In the claiming process, the following three groups will be given priority first.

  • Apps from developers
  • Apps made with Pi App Studio that have gained a certain level of support
  • Shops that need an online presence (a place on the internet where people can find them)

These groups show the clearest path to being connected to a use right away. The wider goal is clear: to prevent large-scale hoarding of domains at low cost, and to help meaningful uses and apps grow.

Domains are an important shared resource in the Pi ecosystem, and using them correctly is important to make the Pi ecosystem stronger and to give Pioneers tools to build, connect, and transact (buy, sell, and exchange) with greater trust and function.
