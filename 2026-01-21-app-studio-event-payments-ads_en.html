Pi App Studio Expands App Utilities in 2026 With a Creator Event, New Payment Feature, and Expanded Access to App Creation

Published: January 21, 2026

Categories: general, update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi App Studio starts 2026 with a combination of a community-wide creator event and new updates. These updates support easy integration (putting something into an app) of Pi payments, help ongoing creation of utility (real usefulness), and widen access to app creation. These changes are designed to support more Pioneers (Pi users) so they can try, improve again and again, and build useful apps that increase Pi's utility.

The first update is that Pioneers can now easily integrate Pi payments into their apps through App Studio, without writing code or doing other technical work. These payments are currently limited to Test-Pi (test-use Pi). The second update is a new option to publish a revised version of an app without paying Pi, by watching ads. This means new Pioneers, Pioneers who have not moved to Mainnet (the real, live network), and Pioneers who do not want to use Pi can also take part in app creation through App Studio. Please note that these ads do not cover the actual cost of publishing an app in Pi App Studio. They are there to widen creator access and lower the cost of revising an app, while preventing spam (unwanted mass activity) and misuse.

Together, these efforts widen access to Pi App Studio, encourage active use, and help Pi cryptocurrency (digital money that uses cryptography, a way to keep information secure) be integrated into app experiences within the Pi ecosystem (the whole set of related apps and services).

App creation event and feedback through a survey

In this event, Pioneers are invited to share their opinions and introduce their favorite App Studio apps that they think are useful, through a short survey. With the first 1,000 eligible responses, respondents can receive 5 Pi credits. These credits can be used only inside Pi App Studio, for app creation and customization (making an app fit one's own needs). Spam responses are not eligible.

Each AI-generated revision and publishing to a server in App Studio costs Pi, so these credits are meant to remove the cost burden when trying. Also, because App Studio is for people who are not technical to create and run apps, it also encourages Pioneers to try app creation themselves, or to revise existing ones further.

The opinions collected through the survey will be used as a guide for future improvements to App Studio's features and tools.

To take part, tap the banner at the top of the App Studio screen.

Easy payment integration in App Studio apps

Pi App Studio now supports integration of in-app payments, using Test-Pi at first. This lets App Studio apps contribute to Pi's utility by integrating Pi payments into the app experience. Integrating Pi payments normally needs developer time and technical knowledge. Even using a recent developer library (a ready-made set of programs that developers can reuse) that shortens the technical integration time for payments to 10 minutes, a developer still needs knowledge and technical skill to complete integrating payments into a Pi app. Pi App Studio has put all of the complexity of Pi payment integration into simple, non-technical, interactive steps inside the app creation workflow (the flow of work), so creators can receive the benefit of Pi payment integration in the same way as developers.

This is an important step not only toward letting non-technical people create and own working apps, but also toward creating a motive for creator monetization (a way to earn money) and growing sustainable business models (ways of running a business that can continue) for App Studio apps. This encourages more people to become app creators, and makes app creation sustainable (able to continue).

In the current version of this feature, creators can add Test-Pi payment interactions that apply during one active session (while the app is being used). For example, buying an item, or unlocking extra features or benefits inside the app. One session means one self-contained activity in an app, such as one round, one task, or one experience.

To integrate payments, Pioneers should first create a new custom app in App Studio in Pi Browser, not in the Pi Desktop app. After the app is created, tap the "Customize App With Pi AI" button, and specifically include the words "Pi payment" in the prompt (the instruction text). Details can be checked in the video instructions.

This early version does not yet support keeping purchases across sessions. That is, if a user leaves and comes back, the purchase is not saved. However, it builds a base for richer app creation opportunities, and prepares creators for Mainnet-ready payments.

App creation in Pi App Studio supported by ads

Pioneers can now publish a revised version of their app in App Studio without paying Pi, by watching ads. With this option, more Pioneers, especially Pioneers who have not yet moved to Mainnet and cannot pay Pi, can try, learn, and create apps without being limited by their migration (moving to Mainnet) status or Pi balance. This feature is available when a Pioneer's balance in App Studio falls below 0.25 Pi.

Please note that the ad revenue from this feature does not cover the costs of app generation and publishing in App Studio, including API costs (API means a connection point that lets systems work together) and server costs (it is not even on the same scale, meaning the amounts are far apart). This approach is designed to intentionally subsidize (partly pay for) the cost of app creation in App Studio, in order to widen access and increase participation in creation, while preventing spam and misuse of the platform. Therefore, this feature, as a support measure to lower creator costs and support ecosystem growth, may be changed or stopped in the future, depending on whether it is economically sustainable.

By asking for opinions, making easy payment integration possible, and widening access to app creation, Pi App Studio continues in 2026 to support the creation of apps with real utility and uses, and supports Pioneers in turning their creativity into app and online business ownership, even if they are not technical. People are invited to go to App Studio today, take part, and turn ideas into actually working apps.
