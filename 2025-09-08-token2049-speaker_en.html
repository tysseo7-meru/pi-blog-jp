Pi Network Founder Chengdiao Fan Confirmed as Speaker at Upcoming TOKEN2049 Conference

Published: September 8, 2025

Categories: announcement

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Dr. Chengdiao Fan, one of the two founders of Pi Network, will be a speaker at the TOKEN2049 event held in Singapore on October 1-2, 2025. In this talk, she will focus on crypto assets and blockchain as tools for real social use and impact. She will analyze the current challenges of Web3 (a decentralized internet built on blockchains) and possible solutions to move the crypto field further toward innovation in real-world use.

As one of the largest crypto events in the world, TOKEN2049 is expected to bring together more than 25,000 participants, 300 speakers, and 500 exhibitors from around the world, including founders, executives, investors, developers, and policymakers. The event is widely seen as a central meeting place for the global Web3 ecosystem, and it includes educational events, networking events, hackathons (app-building competitions), and opportunities to grow businesses.

With Dr. Fan speaking at this event and Pi Network serving as a gold sponsor of TOKEN2049, Pi's activity ensures that the network has a strong presence on one of the industry's largest stages. These steps increase Pi's visibility throughout the conference. They show the project's intention to engage directly with the global Web3 community, and also to engage with the Pi community through this additional channel. Dr. Fan being among the speakers is also a special opportunity to share Pi's view on real-world use, community building, and the wider future of blockchain adoption.

As the event approaches, details of Dr. Fan's talk will be shared through Pi Network's official channels. For now, the confirmation of both the sponsorship and the founder's participation is an important step as Pi deepens its engagement with the wider blockchain ecosystem.
