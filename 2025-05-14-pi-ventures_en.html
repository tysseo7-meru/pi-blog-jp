Launching Pi Network Ventures: Investing $100M in Innovative Startups

Published: May 14, 2025

Categories: announcement

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The Pi Foundation announced the official start of Pi Network Ventures. This is a 100 million dollar development effort, in Pi and US dollars, that strategically invests in startups (new companies) and businesses, including those that advance Pi's usefulness, adoption, and real-world impact.

Pi Network Ventures is set up through the Pi Foundation. The Pi Foundation is an organization without an owner, whose purpose is to guide the long-term development of the Pi ecosystem (the group of apps and services related to Pi) and to support the network's sustainability and functions into the future. The Pi set aside for this effort comes from 10% of the tokens specially allocated as the Foundation's reserve. Now that the Open Network (the production network connected to the outside) has begun and external connections are enabled, this step opens the door to creating high-impact uses and to real-world integration.

Pi Network Ventures is a strategic effort focused on turning Pi from a widely held token into a widely used token.

The aims of Pi Network Ventures

Pi Network Ventures was created with three central goals.

  • Increase what Pi can be used for: support startups and businesses that build Pi into their own products, services, and customer experiences
  • Bring more of the world's production onto Pi Network: invest early in startups and companies that create real-world production processes, whatever form they take — apps, transactions, or company forms — and put those processes on Pi
  • Bring Pi into the real world: support real-world use cases and applications that show Pi's unique value as a widely spread, easy-to-use, and usable crypto asset

By providing motivation and resources, aligned, to promising founders, startups, and companies, this effort aims to create a system in which innovation and adoption go in a cycle. It is designed to benefit builders, businesses, and, most importantly, the community of Pioneers around the world.

Pi Network Ventures is part of Pi's diverse, high-standard strategy to increase uses and support the Pi ecosystem. High-profile investment and incubation work in parallel with grassroots use creation and ecosystem activity. High-profile investment and incubation usually support a small number of high-quality startups and businesses, based on very selective criteria. Grassroots activity, on the other hand, uses Pi's existing community and can produce a large number of use-creation attempts at a large scale. Pi uses both strategies. Pi Network Ventures belongs to the first, and hackathons (events where developers gather for a short period and build works) and every other foundation for scaling up use creation belong to the second.

Supporting builders from the start to scale

Pi Network Ventures is considering investments in a wide range of companies, from early-stage startups to ventures at Series B (a funding round at the stage where a business has entered its growth stage) and beyond. This wide range increases the chances to reach not only promising innovators, but also businesses that have widened their scale and have a track record, and each increases the opportunities to raise Pi's usefulness.

Pi Network Ventures differs from most crypto asset ecosystem programs in at least two important points. First, blockchain-based startups are included among the investment targets, but the investment also puts great weight on the general technology field. It includes generative AI (artificial intelligence that creates text, images, and the like), AI applications, FinTech (the field combining finance and technology), embedded payments, e-commerce foundations, marketplaces (places that connect sellers and buyers), social networks, and real-world consumer and business applications.

From the world's e-commerce foundations that accept Pi, to AI-enabled services that use Pi for access and credit, to traditional businesses that build Pi into loyalty programs (systems of benefits based on use), the Pi Foundation intends to explore investment opportunities in companies that can advance real-world uses and the progress of the ecosystem.

Second, the investment process of Pi Network Ventures is intended to mirror the ways of traditional Silicon Valley venture capital (companies that invest in new companies expected to grow), in matters such as finding targets, selection, and review processes. It is designed to find and support startups and businesses that have a large impact and change existing ways of doing things.

Strategic benefits for Pioneers

Pi Network Ventures was created with the Pioneer community at its center. According to the Pi Foundation, the benefits for Pioneers and the community are as follows.

  • Your own uses for Pi increase: the most important benefit is that uses increase. The more businesses accept Pi and build it into real applications such as products, services, transactions, and experiences, the more diverse the ways you can use Pi become
  • A stronger ecosystem: when businesses take in Pi, they bring new users and use cases. This creates natural growth while keeping use aligned with real activity and usefulness, and makes a stronger ecosystem. A stronger ecosystem increases the opportunities for Pioneers to take part as users, builders, workers, and entrepreneurs
  • Motivation for high-quality integration: the Pi Foundation focuses on investing in promising startups and top-level businesses, across fields and regions, including those that build in Pi. This stance attracts builders, increases scale, widens the network's outlook, and speeds up adoption in a strategic and sustainable way
  • The network effect at work: every business that joins the ecosystem can add value to all other participants. Pioneers can use new services, developers gain more tools and ideas, and businesses gain access to Pi's user base. This accumulating growth effect helps Pi move from a newly opened network to a widely accepted standard for decentralized uses

Access to one of the world's largest crypto asset user bases

Companies that Pi Network Ventures may invest in may be able to obtain something very rare, both in the crypto asset field and in traditional venture capital. Funding comes together with access to a market, and this can address two elements often said to be essential in building a startup. Investee companies will have the opportunity to access the Pi Network user base of tens of millions of people across more than 200 countries and regions — a large, active user base that has completed KYC (identity verification) — of whom more than 19 million people have already completed identity verification through Pi KYC.

This worldwide user base is not only an asset for businesses; it can also become a competitive strength. It may enable growth through the network, systems where customer feedback goes in a cycle, and unique token systems that would not be possible elsewhere.

How the investment proceeds

Investments are generally expressed in US dollar amounts, but Pi Network Ventures aims to make most of its investments directly in the form of Pi itself. Companies joining the ecosystem will be motivated by the very currency they themselves are strengthening and growing.

Note that the Pi Network Ventures investment is intended to provide both funding and strategic value, over a broader period, to partners considered to be in the best position to create value in the world and increase the ways Pi is used worldwide. Depending on the quality of applicants and the number of startups accepted into this effort, the Pi Foundation is not obliged to invest the full 100 million dollars. Pi Network Ventures may invest over time, and may stop investing at any time.

The turning point of the Open Network

The start of the Open Network took Pi into a new era. Now, with Pi Network Ventures in place, the Pi Foundation can put even more effort into its mission of supporting and growing Pi's worldwide uses.

By investing in startups and businesses, Pi aims to support many developers who build with Pi, integrate it, and innovate with it, and to bring real-world uses. Pi Network Ventures investments seek innovators who focus on plans that can be used from around the world, are community-led, and are economically meaningful.
