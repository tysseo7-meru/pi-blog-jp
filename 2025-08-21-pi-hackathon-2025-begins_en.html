Pi Hackathon 2025 Has Officially Started!

Published: August 21, 2025

Categories: hackathon

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi announced the official start of Pi Hackathon 2025 (a competition to build apps). This is the first hackathon in the era of Open Network (a public network connected to the outside). This is an exciting moment for the Pi community. It gives developers a chance to build Pi apps for real use that bring large practical value to millions of everyday Pioneers (members of Pi) and users around the world, including people who have never mined Pi (collected Pi through the app). The hackathon starts today and will run until October 15, 2025.

Since registration began last week (August 15), the situation is as follows.

  • More than 2,100 developers from more than 100 countries and regions have registered to take part.
  • More than 900 developers are still looking for teammates.

With total prizes of 160,000 Pi prepared, this hackathon is designed to draw out innovation (new ideas and new ways of doing things), cooperation, and creativity. Whether you are an experienced builder or have just started, this is a chance to have a lasting effect on the Pi system by building apps that solve real problems and expand the everyday uses of Pi. Participants can use Pi App Studio (a tool for building Pi apps) to build apps easily and improve them quickly through repeated changes. They can also use the Brainstorm app in Pi Browser to share prototypes (early test versions of an app), connect with other people, and become more visible inside the Pi community.

Participants are asked not to forget to join the Google group (an online group for sharing messages) in order to receive important notices and updates.

Full details on the theme, prizes, rules, and schedule can be checked in the full announcement. To take part, you should start by registering. The article calls on people to create new ideas together and raise the uses of Pi to the next level.
