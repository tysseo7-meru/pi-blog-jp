Pi Open Network: One Year In!

Happy first Open Network anniversary, Pioneers. The Open Network, launched on February 20, 2025, was a turning point in Pi's history. It connected Pi's blockchain, its identity-verified community, and its Web3 ecosystem (a decentralized web that uses blockchains) with the outside world.

Before the Open Network, the Pi community spent six years building the ecosystem together, to ensure Pi's readiness and sustainable utility. This period of development let Pi create real apps and utility that Pioneers can use, verify the identity of millions of Pioneers, and prepare the network to handle real-world assets and production systems.

From the beginning, Pi was designed to be freely accessible. Anyone could mine (the action of earning Pi in the app) without technical or financial barriers. That design not only allowed wide adoption and open participation. It also allowed the network and all participants to work patiently on the difficult task of building a fully working ecosystem based on utility.

Since then, the network has kept progressing in line with Pi's long-term vision and strategy: the goal of creating a cryptocurrency that is widely accessible to everyone, open, based on utility, and widely adopted. Progress in the first year of the Open Network includes advances in Pi's ecosystem activity, app development efforts and tools, and the release of innovative products and utility across the platform. Main examples include Pi App Studio, Pi Network Ventures, and the DEX / AMM / token creation features. For more background, you can refer to the First 100 Days of Open Network and the 2025 Year-End updates blogs.

To mark this milestone, Pi's founders, Nicolas Kokkalis and Chengdiao Fan, answered a series of questions. They gave Pioneers a detailed view of Pi's strategy, approach, and current work since the Open Network, and showed more clearly the direction and priorities that will guide future development. This blog gives a rough outline and shares how Pioneers can join the celebration. The video deals with the following questions in detail.

The outlook for the next year

Pi's approach — focusing on utility, large-scale adoption, verified identity, and ease of use — shows a unique form for succeeding with the general public, and makes Pi different from the blockchain industry as a whole.

Over the next year, Pi will focus on widening opportunities to create apps and utility, and on strengthening the tools that support innovation across the whole ecosystem. Together with that, Pi will also raise the speed of processes that matter to Pioneers, such as KYC (identity check) and Mainnet migration. The work includes combining Web3 tools with innovation across products, addressing crypto challenges such as digital identity, helping interoperability (the ability of different systems to work together), and improving the experience of developers, businesses, and Pioneers overall.

By making it easy to build, participate, and create, Pi keeps evolving an ecosystem that is accessible and based on utility.

In this blog, Pi introduces two of the many efforts it is working on to strengthen the network. Pioneers are again encouraged to watch the full video, which covers these topics in more detail.

Ecosystem token design on the Mainnet

Pi ecosystem tokens are tokens created by the community and issued on Pi. They are designed to support real utility and product innovation. Pi stresses that the network design of ecosystem tokens — that is, how ecosystem tokens are built into the Pi ecosystem and how they are used to support real products and services — is the most important factor in enabling ecosystem token creation on the Mainnet (the live blockchain).

Across the whole crypto field, the gap between token issuance and real innovation is a central problem. Even though tokens are a powerful feature in Web3, most tokens on other crypto networks function mainly as tools for raising funds. However, such projects often cannot provide meaningful utility and useful products. The gap between issuing tokens and creating products is a structural problem in Web3.

Pi is in a good position to show a different path, because the network has shared resources, such as a large community with real users who use real products. Pi's approach is to build crypto tokens directly into the process of product and innovation development. For this, Pi's current launch program (a support system for starting) recommends a new design system based on Pi's existing resources and base. It is a utility token designed for user acquisition. In other words, a project issues a token not to raise funds, but to meet its need to gain users for its own product, and builds these tokens into uses inside the product. Users can hold projects to account for the products they offer, give feedback to improve the products, and obtain the utility tokens that projects issue in a transparent way, based on ability and track record. Pi is staked (set aside) and used to adjust and smoothly run these systems.

You can watch the video in which the Pi founders set out the design philosophy behind ecosystem tokens, or read the video's script.

The main distinguishing features of this new system are as follows.

  • All of the consideration in Pi that participants set out to obtain tokens is not transferred to the project issuing the token, unlike in the usual form of token issuance. Instead, all of the consideration is deposited, in a way that cannot be undone, into a liquidity pool (a collection of funds that makes trading go smoothly), supporting the token's stability and soundness
  • A project that issues a token through this new launch system must, as a condition for issuing a token through this program, have a real working product in the form of a functioning app
  • When participants engage with a project's product, they may be given favorable terms or benefits at the launch

Pi has published the specific details of the ecosystem token design on Pi Network as a Pi Request for Comment (PiRC, a document asking for comments) to the community. Given that the ecosystem token layer is important for the Pi ecosystem and that its approach and design are innovative, such a design should be evaluated with comments from the community and improved repeatedly in practice. It should be noted that this design is intentionally different from existing forms, in order to address the problems explained earlier. It aims to align the powerful Web3 feature of tokens with the creation of real-world products and innovation, and it uses Pi Network's existing strengths and resources.

Pioneers can read the full details of the ecosystem token design on GitHub. Comments are welcome. Readers are encouraged to use GitHub features such as issues (posts about problems) and pull requests (proposals to change the text or code) to share specific suggestions about particular parts of the design and to discuss them with other community members. If you do not have a GitHub account, a Google Form is also available for submitting general comments.

Pi will review and consider the comments from GitHub and the general comments submitted through the Google Form. As with any design process, not all suggestions will be adopted, but comments will be evaluated to judge whether adjustments are appropriate. The design of this system may also be revised further, based on feedback from projects that wish to take part in the launch program.

Widening the usefulness of Pi KYC

Pi KYC is the foundation of the network's integrity and authenticity. Seen more broadly, Pi views identity verification as an important unsolved challenge in Web3, and as a step needed before large-scale real-world production moves on-chain (onto the blockchain).

However, beyond its use inside Pi Network, the next stage of this work is to treat KYC itself as a service — that is, KYC as a platform capability. Identity is the foundation for meaningful integration with the real-world economy. Every transfer of value and ownership needs to know who is sending and who is receiving. This is a fundamental identity problem, and therefore a KYC problem.

In the near future, Pi will offer the technology and product of the Pi KYC solution as a service for Web3 and traditional businesses, without sharing any user data. Pi's unique approach to KYC offers third parties the following unique benefits.

  • Coverage across the world
  • Scalability (the ability to grow in size)
  • A hybrid form that combines AI and human validation work
  • Sanctions checks for legal compliance
  • Comparison across networks
  • Support for different forms of regulation

Seen over a longer term, the identity-verified network itself is designed so that services and use cases can be built and integrated on top of it, as a powerful shared resource for the whole network. Users keep managing their own identity and data themselves, but they also gain the choice to take part in apps built on the identity-verified network. That network provides utility to outside services that need the participation of identity-verified people.

This matters far beyond Pi. Solving KYC is a precondition for many of the main challenges in Web3, including real-world assets. Seen more broadly, in an AI-driven world filled with bots and agents that can imitate human behavior more and more, being able to answer the simple but important question of who is real becomes essential.

What this first anniversary means

The first anniversary of Pi's Open Network looks back on the contributions of Pioneers around the world. From building apps, strengthening the base, verifying identities, and taking part in ecosystem activities, to supporting community growth, Pioneer participation has been essential in shaping Pi as it is today.

Pi has always been a joint effort. The continued progress of the ecosystem will be driven in the future, too, by the cooperation of developers, businesses, and Pioneers working together to create meaningful utility and real-world adoption. This is a path that others did not choose.

Thank you to every Pioneer who has contributed to this unique journey and keeps shaping what comes next.
