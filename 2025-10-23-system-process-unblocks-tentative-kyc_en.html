System Process Checks Allow Over 3.36 Million Tentatively KYC’d Pioneers to Fully Pass Pi KYC

Published: October 23, 2025

Categories: update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

A recently released system process has newly allowed more than 3.36 million Pioneers to fully pass KYC (identity verification). This process runs additional checks on tentative KYC cases (a temporary state placed before full approval). More than 4.76 million Pioneers with tentative KYC became eligible to complete KYC in full.

This large-scale system process includes a complex system that uses advanced AI models to analyze large amounts of liveness check data (checks that confirm the person is a real, living person) and KYC application data. It is designed to confirm both that each applicant is a real, living person and that they have passed the additional checks needed to fully pass KYC.

Together, these checks stop fraudulent accounts from passing KYC, protect Pi's policy of one account per person, and further protect the health of the network and fairness for honest, real Pioneers.

Of the more than 3.36 million who fully passed KYC, about 2.69 million have already migrated to the Mainnet (the live network) blockchain. Those who have not yet migrated to Mainnet are asked to check whether they have fully passed KYC, complete the Mainnet Checklist, and migrate.

In addition, there are 3 million Pioneers with tentative KYC cases. If these people submit the additional liveness check requested in the app, they may be able to pass through this new system process. If your KYC status is tentative and the app asks for an additional liveness check, you are asked to complete the liveness check as soon as possible. Doing so makes you eligible for this system process and brings your KYC application closer to a final decision.

In addition to completing the required tasks and checklists, actively mining (the act of earning Pi in the app) and using the Pi app can trigger many of the automated system processes behind the scenes, including the process released this time. For that reason, it may help speed up KYC and migration processing.

This progress answers the needs of Pioneers in a tentative KYC state, and further advances the foundation of Pi Network's Web3 (a decentralized internet built on blockchains): a safe, genuine, and open network made up of real individuals.
