New Apps Listed in Mainnet Ecosystem Interface & Update to Account Recovery Options

Published: May 23, 2025

Categories: update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

This article introduces two recent updates that show what is progressing in the Pi ecosystem (the set of apps and services connected with Pi). The first is that new community apps were added to the Mainnet ecosystem interface. The second is that a new account recovery flow was introduced, so that Pioneers can reset their passwords safely and efficiently.

New apps joined the Mainnet ecosystem interface

A new batch of Pi apps was added to the Mainnet ecosystem interface. The five apps include a snake game app, an e-commerce app (for buying and selling goods on the internet), and an app that provides information about the Pi token. They met the network's standards on app quality, completeness of features, evaluation of their use, and fit with the stricter Pi ecosystem policies and guidelines.

New apps being listed not only widens what Pioneers can use today; it also becomes a stronger example for future developers. As more apps are added and published, the ecosystem becomes a more attractive and rewarding place for community developers to build, creating good momentum toward innovation, adoption, and real-world use.

Note that the Mainnet app list does not include all Mainnet apps and Mainnet-ready apps. Listing is limited, based on quality, the use provided, and fit with Pi ecosystem policies and guidelines. There are many other third-party Pi apps that are not listed here. Some third-party Pi apps that show lists of Pi Mainnet apps display more of them, or all of them, and Pioneers can refer to those to see the available apps more widely.

A new account recovery option is available

A new flow was introduced for password resets. If a Pioneer has set a trusted email address, a password reset link is sent to that email address, in addition to the existing methods.

This added option aims to make account recovery easier to use and more reliable, while keeping it safe. Recovery by email is simpler and cheaper for users, and is generally reliable, for example because it does not depend on a local telecom provider. This not only protects each person's access to Pi; by letting more people stay connected to the ecosystem and keep taking part, it also strengthens participation across the whole network.
