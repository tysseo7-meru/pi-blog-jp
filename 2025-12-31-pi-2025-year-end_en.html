A Look Back at 2025: Open Network, Real-World Utility, and an Expansive Ecosystem

Published: December 31, 2025

Categories: general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

2025 was a milestone year for Pi Network. In February, the Open Network (a state where the network can connect with outside networks) started. Pi's system became able to engage with the wider blockchain world. This was a point that Pi reached because it spent more than 6 years building its infrastructure (the basic systems) and its community, and because real uses were ready at the time of the start.

Updates up to 100 days after the start of the Open Network have already been reported in the article '100 Days of Open Network'. This year-end look back focuses on the main releases and progress after that. This year's milestones covered progress in AI tools and infrastructure, investment by Pi Network Ventures that expands the system, improvements in KYC and migration that let people take part sooner and more widely, ongoing Node and protocol (rules for communication and processing) updates, and wider participation and uses.

AI, Creation, and Human Participation

As AI infrastructure moves forward, Pi looked at challenges that are not yet solved. They are: the layer of apps that use AI is still at an early stage; the role that human intelligence plays in a world centered on AI; and how to distribute fairly, to real people, the benefits of production created by AI. Pi sees AI and blockchain together as one system. It supports productivity, being real, responsibility, and delivering AI production widely to people through blockchain.

  • To grow an active and rich layer of AI apps, Pi App Studio was released. It is a creation tool that uses generative AI (AI that creates text, images, or code). Even without technical knowledge, you can build an app and release it. Steps are provided to turn knowledge of a field and ideas into products that can be released directly inside Pi's system. More than 13,400 chatbot apps and more than 24,400 custom apps were built and released. More than 51,800 Pioneers built apps, including unpublished plans
  • Pi App Studio was updated. Creators can now download code directly inside App Studio, change it for their own use, and upload it again

Pi Network Ventures

Pi Network Ventures is an investment effort at the scale of 100 million dollars, started by the Pi Foundation. Its funds are in Pi and in US dollars. It invests with a clear aim in startups and businesses that advance Pi's uses, spread, and real-world impact. Two investments have been announced.

  • The first investment is OpenMind. It is a company that builds an operating system (basic software) and an open-source protocol for robots to think, learn, and work together. It is also called 'Android for robots'. As part of this cooperation, Pi's worldwide Node network was used in a proof-of-concept test (a test to check whether an idea can work in practice), to see whether it can support decentralized AI training and computing work
  • The second investment is CiDi Games. It is a gaming platform company that builds games connected with Pi that tens of millions of Pioneers can use. It also has a role in expanding Pi's infrastructure so that it can support other games and developers. Games fit Pi's system in terms of creating uses and participation, so Pi continues to grow the gaming field. This partnership is also part of that work

KYC and Mainnet Migration

The steps for KYC and Mainnet (the live network) migration were improved. The focus was on removing barriers to participation, and on letting more Pioneers engage with the uses of the system.

  • Fast Track KYC (a faster route for identity verification) was released. New Pioneers and people who had not been users can now take part in the Mainnet system at an earlier stage than before
  • Through a system verification process, more than 3.36 million users with tentative KYC fully passed KYC. The number of tentative KYC Pioneers who were covered was more than 4.76 million
  • With the additional introduction of AI, the standard KYC process became faster and easier to expand in scale, while keeping accuracy. The queue of cases waiting for human verifiers to look at and judge fell by 50%. The blockage on the verifier side eased. The processing time for standard KYC may become shorter

At present, more than 17.5 million Pioneers have fully passed KYC, and 15.8 million people have migrated to Mainnet.

Node and Protocol Updates

On Testnet (the test network), there are more than 350,000 Nodes (computers that take part in the network), and more than 1 million CPUs. Updates focused on supporting people who operate Nodes, and on preparing for future connections and uses.

  • Node version updates (from 0.5.2 to 0.5.4) improved overall security
  • The name of the Pi Node app was changed to 'Pi Desktop'. This was to make it a more general app, open also to current and future connections and apps on the desktop that are not limited to Nodes
  • A Node ranking page was released. It shows the top 5,000 Pi Nodes, based on main indicators such as reliability, whether the Node is available, open ports (open connection points), total active days, and CPU performance
  • Public keys were made visible to anyone, so Pioneers can find their own Node on the ranking page. A public key can be used to verify and identify a user's Node
  • The Pi App Studio screen was made usable inside the Pi Node / Desktop app, and it was improved
  • The system for tracking open ports on Pioneers' Nodes was improved
  • Multiple bugs and problems reported by the community were fixed
  • With the release of Pi Linux Node, partners and Pioneers with technical knowledge can run Node software on Linux that is standardized and updates automatically. This moved forward consistency of operations, ease of use, and preparation of long-term infrastructure
  • Protocol updates moved forward from version 19 to version 23. New functions and controls are added to Pi's protocol
  • A new Node use is being considered, where Pi's worldwide Node network supports decentralized AI training and computing work, in addition to its role of protecting Pi's ledger (the record of transactions). The idea is that the side that needs computing power to train AI models pays Node operators in Pi for additional capacity. As stated earlier, Pi completed a proof-of-concept test to run computations for OpenMind, and has started to explore applications of this use

Ecosystem Utility and Participation

Pi continued to support engagement with the system, by supporting app building, making practical uses possible, and strengthening participation across the whole network.

  • Ecosystem Directory Staking is a platform-level utility (a basic use at the level of the whole system). It is a system where Pioneers and businesses can support and promote, by themselves and in a decentralized way, the ranking of Pi apps and functions on the ecosystem screen
  • Pi Hackathon 2025 was the first hackathon (a development event where apps are built in a short time) after the start of the Open Network. It called on developers to build and release meaningful Pi apps that strengthen real-world uses with Pi. From more than 215 completed Mainnet apps that were submitted, 3 winners and 5 honorable mentions were chosen
  • The auction for .pi domains ended, and the next steps to receive a domain were shown. The steps to receive a domain are to make sure that domains are used for a real purpose, and to make the risk of malicious buying-up (buying many names to hold them) smaller
  • With the release on Testnet of DEX (decentralized exchange, an exchange with no central operator), AMM (a system that decides prices automatically and exchanges assets) liquidity pools (pools of assets set aside for exchange), and token creation functions, developers and Pioneers gained a real environment where they can safely try the workings of decentralized finance (DeFi, finance done without an intermediary) inside Pi's system, before deployment to Mainnet. These functions recently received an update to improve ease of use and clarity, based on first use, testing, and user feedback
  • Chengdiao Fan, one of the two Pi founders, spoke at TOKEN2049 in Singapore. In the presentation, she explored in particular how blockchain can advance real societal uses and impact in the age of AI. She showed the current challenges of Web3 (a decentralized web based on blockchain) and possible solutions to move the crypto asset field forward toward innovation in real-world uses, rather than looking only at liquidity (how easily an asset can be bought and sold)

Pi Network in 2026: More Building, Utilities, and Use Cases

Through the community's efforts this year, the start of the Open Network became possible, together with many apps, functions, and uses that will continue to be built in the future. Pi Network in 2026 will be shaped by Pi's long-term direction and plans, and by Pioneers, developers, and partners building and using the system together.

As the year comes to an end, Pi continues to focus on steady progress, responsible building, and creating long-term uses. The article closes with thanks to all Pioneers, and with greetings for the new year.
