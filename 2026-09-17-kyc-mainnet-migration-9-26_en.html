KYC & Mainnet Migration Updates: Resolving Additional Corner Cases That Block KYC Application & Migration Progress & Improving Processes

Published: September 17, 2026

Categories: general, update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi released several technical updates to the processes for KYC (identity check) and Mainnet migration (moving a balance to the live network). They cover special cases in KYC applications, improvements to the checking process, and help for Pioneers who could not move forward before.

KYC and Mainnet migration include various checks. These checks let real Pioneers move forward while stopping bad actors, bots (automatic programs), and people who broke the rules. Most Pioneers keep moving through these processes, but certain groups can get stuck because of special cases that need extra evaluation or a dedicated technical fix.

Clearing the special KYC cases flagged as possible duplicate accounts

Pi reviewed the process for evaluating a special KYC case: Pioneers whose accounts were flagged as possibly being duplicates. The extra evaluation confirmed that they were not duplicates, so more than 417,000 Pioneers can now move forward in the KYC process.

Note that this does not replace the other KYC requirements and checks that apply. The Pioneers concerned still need to meet them.

This update is one example of why some groups of Pioneers are unblocked in batches rather than one by one. In special cases, extra analysis and a dedicated technical fix may be needed to tell real Pioneers apart from bad actors and rule-breakers. Each group is stuck for a different reason. When a fix for a specific problem is built, tested, and released, the Pioneers it covers can move forward.

Unblocking Mainnet migration for Pioneers with Fast-Track wallets

Some Pioneers received a Mainnet wallet through Fast-Track (a shortcut process), and that wallet later became the destination wallet for their Mainnet migration. Because these wallets were not created through the standard path of the usual KYC and migration process, they did not have enough Pi balance to cover the gas fees (the fees needed to process a transaction), so they could not receive their migrated balance.

Within the next week, an update will be released to unblock the 497,000 Pioneers stuck on this problem. The update also aligns the gap between the two KYC paths that lead to migration, so that similar cases do not happen in the future.

Further improvements to KYC and Mainnet migration

Several other improvements were also released, addressing specific problems across KYC and migration. They are as follows.

  • Technical processes were optimized to reduce errors: technical improvements were made to how machine learning models for video processing and other automated KYC processes use system resources in the KYC app. This addresses cases where heavy processing load caused errors and stopped KYC applications
  • KYC resubmission for affected Yoti users: some Pioneers who used the early KYC process through Yoti were stuck because the data from the required liveness check (a check that the person is present in person) was missing. These Pioneers can now resubmit their KYC application to add the missing information
  • More devices can now do the liveness check: the liveness check and the face capture at different angles were adjusted. Pioneers using older or lower-performance phones, who could not finish the check because of their device's limits, are now covered
  • Palm print capture as an additional identity check: for some Pioneers who need to complete multiple liveness checks, palm print capture was enabled for a one-month trial period. This feature is being rolled out in stages. The extra identity data can help verify real Pioneers and support clearing Tentative KYC status. Because palm prints do not require showing one's face, they are another way to support proof that a person is human while protecting privacy in a different way
  • Resubmission for affected Indonesian ID holders: Indonesian Pioneers who submitted a KIA identity card (an ID that is no longer valid for KYC) and were rejected can now resubmit using another supported identity document

These updates are part of Pi's ongoing work on specific special cases in KYC and Mainnet migration. They help the Pioneers concerned keep moving forward, while keeping the checks needed to identify genuine human participants.

Pioneers are encouraged to complete liveness checks when asked, to open the KYC app and check whether they can resubmit, and, if a migration failed before because the balance could not cover the gas fee, to try receiving their Pi again.
