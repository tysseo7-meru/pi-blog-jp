Pioneers, Help Bring More AI-Created Apps to Pi and Enter Raffle to Win Pi Merchandise!

Published: June 5, 2026

Categories: general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

If you want to help grow the Pi ecosystem, you were asked to invite vibe coders (people who build apps with AI) and bring AI-created apps into Pi's real distribution network and utility ecosystem through Pi App Studio. Pioneers who take part by following the steps below can also enter a raffle to win Pi Network merchandise.

This campaign is built on the recent Pi App Studio release. Through that release, vibe coders can use Pi App Studio to easily convert their apps, built through platforms such as Codex, Claude Code, Replit, Cursor, Lovable, and other AI-assisted coding tools, into Pi apps.

Even as AI makes app creation easier, creators still need users, payment infrastructure, and a place where their app is discovered and used. Pi helps play that role by connecting AI-created apps easily with millions of active Pioneers, identity verification, and Pi's utility ecosystem.

How to take part

Pioneers can take part by joining relevant creator and developer communities and sharing why Pi is useful for creators who have already built prototypes or working apps on AI platforms.

  • Find active online platforms and communities of vibe coders. Such vibe coder communities include subreddits, X Communities, Facebook groups, and developer forums.
  • Join that specific vibe coder community and contribute to it. If you are already a member of a relevant vibe coder community, it is even better to start there. If it is your first time, start by engaging with community members on topics around vibe coding and app creation.
  • Introduce Pi Network to vibe coders who are looking for users for their apps, as a distribution network. After finding and joining a relevant community, introduce Pi and what Pi offers. For the suggested wording and resources, see the section below.
  • Submit the link to your post through the "Vibe Coder" button in the Pi mining app. Eligible posts must be accessible, relevant to the community, and in line with the campaign's purpose and the guidelines shown in the original article. Spam-like posts, misleading posts, and unrelated submissions will be disqualified. You may submit only one post.

Pioneers can take part in the campaign and enter the raffle until Pi2Day, June 28, 2026.

How to introduce Pi Network to vibe coder communities

In a post introducing Pi, you can point vibe coders to Pi App Studio, explain how apps built outside can connect with Pi, and highlight the users, payments, advertising platform, and wider infrastructure available through the Pi ecosystem.

Pi's value proposal needs to be clear. In the Pi ecosystem, a vibe coder who builds web apps with AI can, just by connecting their service or product to Pi Network, make their app available to 60M+ (more than 60 million) active users, and run the app with payment, identity verification, and advertising infrastructure built in.

Pi App Studio provides a ready-to-copy-and-paste AI prompt (a written instruction for AI) that vibe coders can use in external AI tools such as Codex, Claude Code, Replit, Cursor, and Lovable. With it, they can integrate the Pi SDK (development tools for connecting with Pi), check the settings, and optionally add Pi payments. This makes it easy for vibe coders and developers who already use AI tools to bring their apps, services, and products into the Pi ecosystem.

Resources such as a guide page for vibe coders, the announcement blog, and video walkthroughs should also be shared.

You can also do the following.

  • Introduce the Pi opportunity to creator communities.
  • Explain how Pi App Studio makes integration with Pi easy for vibe coders.
  • Share statistics on the natural reach Pi apps have gained from the Pi ecosystem, such as the recently reported beta app release cases.
  • Explain why developers and creators should consider Pi.

As a note, Pioneers need to take part in communities in a natural way, follow each community's rules, understand its norms, and contribute in substance. Share Pi only when it is relevant to the conversation, and keep posts factual, respectful, and helpful. Do not copy and paste the same general wording into many communities. Do not spam, flood forums with many posts, take over unrelated conversations, or try to dominate other communities. The goal is to help creators discover a genuine opportunity while respecting the places where creators gather.

Growing the Pi ecosystem together

By helping more creators discover Pi, Pioneers help widen the range of apps, tools, and services available in the Pi ecosystem. As the app ecosystem grows stronger, Pioneers get more ways to use Pi, ways to support useful products, and ways to take part in utility.

By sharing Pi Platform and Pi App Studio with relevant creator communities to take part in the campaign, and then submitting the link to your public post in the Pi app, you can get a chance to win Pi Network merchandise.

As a disclaimer: by submitting a valid post link, you are deemed to have accepted and agreed to take part in the raffle. The 100 winners will be selected through a raffle system at the sole discretion of the Pi Core Team. The raffle prizes cannot be transferred and cannot be resold. Receiving Pi-branded merchandise does not grant or imply any official relationship with, representation of, or endorsement by the Pi Core Team.
