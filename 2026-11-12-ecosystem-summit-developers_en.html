Why Build on Pi: Developer Opportunities in the AI Era

Published: November 12, 2026

Categories: developer, general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

As AI makes software development much more accessible, reaching users and keeping them using an app are becoming more important challenges for developers. Pi addresses these challenges in two ways: by providing tools that make app development and integration easy, and by providing access to an existing ecosystem where developers can connect their products with real users.

These advantages will be the central focus of Pi Network's first-ever Ecosystem Summit, held from November 17 to 19, 2026.

Pi gives developers access to a global community of more than 60 million engaged Pioneers (Pi users), an ecosystem built on a distributed base of people, and a network of more than 420,000 Nodes. Pi's platform also provides developers with features such as user authentication, Pi payments, AI-ready development, monetization, and app discovery. This helps deliver apps to the established community and use Pi's gathered resources to build useful, engaging apps.

Building and growing apps on Pi

These features are available through the Pi SDK (software development kit), the Developer Portal, the Pi Ad Network, App Studio, SoloHost, and more.

Beyond development, Pi also provides systems for apps to reach users and support sustainable business models. Pi Browser lets Pioneers discover and access ecosystem apps. Ecosystem Directory Staking (a system where people stake Pi to support apps, which affects their ranking in the app directory) lets developers gain support from users, raise their ranking in the app directory, and get more exposure and momentum. The Pi Ad Network also provides eligible developers with advertising monetization opportunities, alongside business models supported by Pi payments.

These features can support a wide range of apps across various industries and uses, from consumer products to professional tools, services, and platforms. Developers can build entirely new apps, or build Pi's platform into existing products, and use its community, payment features, and other resources without having to develop each element themselves.

Pi's wider approach to AI

The impact of AI does not stop at making software development easier. As AI's abilities grow, challenges also arise: real human participation, the future of human work, and how individuals contribute to, and benefit from, an economy that becomes more AI-driven.

Pi's approach is to recognize and use the unavoidable changes AI brings to productivity and production, while building things that can serve as solutions to overcome and soften the challenges AI brings. Specifically, this includes encouraging more use of AI in production processes and recording those processes on the blockchain, while at the same time providing the platform and base needed for distributed participation in an AI-driven economy.

Pi Network founder Chengdiao Fan discusses these challenges in more depth, including how AI will reshape work, ownership, and economic participation, in a contribution to TIME Africa's 'Africa Is on the Move (Part III)'.

For example, Pi's identity verification base, with more than 18 million identity-verified Pioneers, provides a foundation for real human participation. Its KYC (identity verification) validator system has already shown that more than 1 million individuals can provide human judgment at large scale. They have completed more than 526 million verification tasks and receive rewards in Pi.

Beyond Pi's own identity-verified community, PiVerify extends identity verification features to third-party companies and apps. It helps solve the challenges of authenticity and identity verification in social interactions and economic transactions where real humans, AI agents, and malicious bots are all active online.

Pi's distributed Node platform and the SoloHost beta provide another direction. They let developers distribute self-run apps such as AI agents, and let individuals explore opportunities connected with computing resources they run themselves.

These features show ways in which AI, blockchain, and human participation complement each other, creating opportunities for people to provide resources and take part in economic activity supported by new technology.

Toward the Pi Ecosystem Summit

The upcoming Pi Ecosystem Summit will introduce Pi's continuing efforts to improve the developer experience and widen opportunities to create utility. Through new platform announcements, technical demonstrations, developer conversations, and app showcases, the summit will be a place to explore how developers can build and grow apps using Pi's platform and its evolving features.
