Pi’s Community Commerce Holiday Initiative and Raffle are Here!

Published: December 18, 2025

Categories: announcement

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

This holiday season, Pi Network is shining a light on commerce apps and local Pi merchants that support Pi commerce.

In this season, large retailers often run promotions, discounts, and holiday shopping benefits. Pi apps and merchants now have a place to do the same, and Pioneers are invited to shop with Pi, both online and in person.

This initiative supports small businesses, local Pi merchants, and Mainnet (live network) commerce apps, and gives the community a simple way to find and join holiday shopping across the ecosystem. Pi Network helps with visibility and coordination, and individual apps and merchants can run their own unique promotions and benefits.

To support this initiative and celebrate the holidays with Pioneers, Pi will hold a raffle for Pi-logo goods (T-shirts and hats). How to take part in both the commerce initiative and the holiday raffle is explained below.

How app developers and merchants can take part

  • Create and manage your own holiday promotions, benefits, and fun plans to gather people who shop with Pi
  • Post about participation and benefits in the PiFest Fireside Forum channel, promote your business, and enter the raffle (details below)
  • Handle sales, delivery, and order fulfillment directly with users

How Pioneers can take part

  • Check the ecosystem guide and Fireside Forum to find participating Pi apps and merchants
  • Shop at a local merchant who accepts Pi, or at a Mainnet commerce app
  • Use the holiday benefits created by merchants and app developers
  • Share your shopping or gift experience in the PiFest Fireside Forum channel and enter the raffle (details below)

Community raffle

You can take part for a chance to win limited Pi goods. Any valid post to the PiFest Fireside Forum channel — whether from a Pioneer shopper, a participating Pi app developer, or a local Pi merchant — automatically enters the raffle. A total of 100 winners will be chosen at random, and they will receive Pi-branded items such as T-shirts and hats. Note that shipping of Pi goods is handled by an outside company, so raffle winners are limited to regions that company's site supports. The support covers more than 220 countries and regions.
