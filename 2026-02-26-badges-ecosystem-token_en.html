Celebrating the Pioneer Community With Pi Badges and Raffle, Ecosystem Token Design Feedback, and Slice of Pi Game!

Published: February 26, 2026

Categories: general, update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Last week marked one year since the start of Open Network (an open state connected to outside networks). This is a milestone to look back on a year that showed growth in utility (being able to actually use something), progress in development, and increased participation by developers and Pioneers (Pi users). On this occasion, Pi's founders shared their views on Pi's strategy, approach, and the direction and priorities of future development.

As the yearly Pi Day on March 14 approaches, as part of community participation and celebration, Pi announces an effort to honor Pioneers' achievements with commemorative badges and hold a raffle for Pi goods. In the weeks leading up to Pi Day, this effort shows the role Pioneers play within the ecosystem (the whole system where apps and services gather), such as using apps, supporting community activity, and taking part in network growth.

Commemorative badges and community raffle

To celebrate Pioneers' achievements in the ecosystem, eligible Pioneers who meet any one of the following conditions will be given a commemorative Pi badge and a chance to take part in a raffle for Pi goods.

  • Pioneers who were among the first 100,000 people to join Pi Network
  • Pioneers who mined (did the action in the app to earn Pi) for 365 days between December 2024 and December 2025
  • Pioneers who spent more than 2,000 hours in Pi Chats between December 2024 and December 2025
  • Pioneers who tried more than 50 Pi apps between December 2024 and December 2025
  • Pioneers who newly joined in 2025 and completed KYC (identity verification)

If any one or more of the above applies, eligible Pioneers will receive a pop-up notice (a message that appears on the screen) after mining. From there, they can receive the badge and choose a badge to display in Pi Chats. Display in Fireside Forum and Pi Social Profiles will be supported within the following week.

If you share the badge on social media, you are automatically entered into the raffle for Pi goods. After receiving the badge, follow the in-app guide to share the badge on social media. After that, submit a link to the post from the in-app steps. Valid submissions will be eligible for a raffle to win Pi-branded goods.

The deadline for raffle entry is 11:59 p.m. (PST, Pacific Standard Time) on March 14, Pi Day. Winners will be 150 people chosen randomly by raffle. Please note that Pi goods are shipped through a third party (another company), so winning is limited to regions that site supports. The supported regions include more than 220 countries and regions. Also, if it is found that a chosen winner submitted invalid content, they will be disqualified, and another winner will be drawn randomly again.

This effort aims to shine light on the varied ways Pioneers contribute to the network, such as participation from the early days, continued mining, exploring the ecosystem, and taking part in the community.

Take part in ecosystem design: check the ecosystem token framework

The design of ecosystem tokens (original tokens made on top of the Pi system) plays a central role in making sure that token creation on Mainnet (the real, live blockchain, which is a system that records transactions in linked blocks) supports real products and sustainable utility. Pi's approach is to integrate cryptocurrency tokens (digital tokens that use cryptography) directly into the process of product and innovation development. In last week's Open Network one-year content, information about ecosystem token design was published in the one-year blog, a video featuring Pi founder Chengdiao Fan, and a Pi Request for Comment (PiRC, a document asking for opinions) to the community.

Pioneers are encouraged to read the full details of the ecosystem token design and PiRC on GitHub (a service for sharing and working on programs together). Opinions are welcome, and people are encouraged to use features such as GitHub issues (posts about problems or tasks) and pull requests (proposals to fix or change something) to share specific suggestions about particular parts of the design and discuss them with other community members. If you do not have a GitHub account, you can submit general opinions through a Google Form.

Pi will check and consider opinions from GitHub and general opinions submitted through the Google Form. As with any design process, not all suggestions will be adopted, but opinions will be evaluated to judge whether adjustments are appropriate. The design system may also be revised further based on opinions from projects that wish to take part in a launch program (a support system for starting).

Slice of Pi: another fun example of Pi utility in games

A third-party game connected with Pi, Slice of Pi, was recently released. In this game, the player is asked to skillfully cut fruit placed on a board above a blender, so that all the fruit falls cleanly into the blender.

Like the FruityPi game released last year, Slice of Pi is a real example showing Pi's game-sector utility actually working. It integrates Pi payments and the Pi Ad Network (Pi's advertising system) into the game experience, and shows that Pi and the utility of the whole Pi platform can support apps as a concrete, sustainable, and open system within the Pi app ecosystem.

Games as a whole are a field where several strengths that Pi is well suited to use meet, such as social interaction, the attention economy (a system where people's attention has value), and digital transactions in an in-game economy. Pi Network Ventures considers games an important field to invest in to increase Pi's utility, and is actively looking for projects that include game design and projects that increase ecosystem utility with full games.

For that reason, developers who make attractive games should connect with Pi and use Pi's base to access resources across the whole Pi ecosystem. If a game gains support in the Pi ecosystem, the developer should also apply to Pi Network Ventures.

Toward Pi Day

Pi Day has consistently been an opportunity to share Pi's big progress, current efforts, and so on. This year's Pi Day follows that flow, and updates will be shared about products, tools, and efforts that advance long-term utility and real-world adoption (people actually starting to use it widely).

As Pi Day approaches, Pioneers are encouraged to take part, keep engaging with the latest ecosystem information, and keep playing an active role in shaping the ecosystem.
