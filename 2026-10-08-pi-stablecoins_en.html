Pi Network’s Approach to Stablecoins

Published: October 8, 2026

Categories: general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi Network's usefulness-centered ecosystem (the whole system in which apps and services gather and work) continues to expand into apps, developer tools, ecosystem-wide services, commerce, payments, and more, increasing the ways Pioneers (Pi users) and businesses can join the network and build on it. Stablecoins (crypto assets that keep their price stable by linking them to things like the US dollar) can play a role in widening the usefulness of the Pi ecosystem, especially in situations where price stability is important.

However, introducing stablecoins requires a careful and original approach, and it must fit the Pi Network ecosystem and its way of thinking. Simply adding a stablecoin does not always mean it fits naturally with Pi. How the two work side by side is decided by how the stablecoin is built into the Pi ecosystem.

For that reason, the design and the way the first introduction is done are especially important. Stablecoin functions should be aimed at situations that need price stability, while the usefulness of the crypto asset Pi should be kept across the whole ecosystem. Because of this, Pi's approach may differ from how stablecoins have been introduced in other places.

The role of stablecoins in the Pi ecosystem

Pi is the native crypto asset of the Pi Network ecosystem, and it supports participation and usefulness across the whole network. A stablecoin is a crypto asset designed to keep a relatively stable value, usually by pegging (linking) it to an asset such as the US dollar. It can play an important role for narrower purposes, where a stable price is especially useful.

When a token's price moves up and down, real difficulties arise for economic activity that assumes predictable prices. Businesses and organizations differ in their ability and willingness to prepare for price changes. If prices are stable, accounting, settlement, and part of the dealings with parties outside the blockchain also become easier.

Stablecoins can also increase the paths that connect to the network, and increase the ways participants can enter the Pi ecosystem. If this function can be used inside the ecosystem, it can support transactions and activities that would otherwise depend on outside systems, and help keep more economic activity inside Pi.

In this way, a stablecoin can widen the kinds of usefulness and economic activity the ecosystem can support, while keeping a role separate from Pi itself. Importantly, Pi remains the central crypto asset in the flow of the whole network, and stablecoins should take a supporting role within the ecosystem.

Working with Open Standard, the company behind the OUSD stablecoin

The reason Pi Network chose Open Standard as the partner to work with on OUSD is that OUSD overlaps with much of the thinking at Pi's foundation. That is, the idea that a network works best when value stays with the people and businesses that create and use it.

OUSD is jointly operated by a growing consortium (a group of companies working together) of more than 200 companies. It is designed so that anyone, regardless of size, can freely issue and redeem (exchange back) it, and so that most of the reserve income is returned to the partners who distribute it. This is a network-type system, and it fits Pi.

In working with OUSD, Pi will explore reward programs for Pioneers and broader usefulness across the Pi ecosystem.

How stablecoins will be introduced

Pi Network recognizes the importance and the unique usefulness of stablecoins in blockchain ecosystems. The specific way stablecoins are introduced within Pi Network will be designed with intent and care, to fit the Pi ecosystem and the unique needs of network participants. The current focus is on settling the thinking that will guide future integration. Following the law is also an important consideration for Pi in making its approach to stablecoin integration.

As Pi continues to build its approach to stablecoins and how they fit the Pi ecosystem, Pi asks people to look forward to future information.
