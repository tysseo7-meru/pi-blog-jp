Announcing Open Network PiFest and New Feature Allowing Phone Number Change With KYC

Published: March 11, 2025

Categories: announcement, pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi Day 2025 (Friday, March 14, 2025) is coming soon. Pi will celebrate with the first Open Network (the live network that can connect to outside systems) PiFest. PiFest connects Pioneers (Pi users) with local shops that accept Pi. With the full support of Open Network connections, PiFest shines a light on uses in local commerce. It shows real use of Pi around the world.

Also, a new feature has been released. Pioneers who have finished KYC (an identity check) can now update their full account phone number if their number has changed or is old. There is an important note about Pi's policy of one account per person. If someone misuses this feature to transfer an account to another person, the original owner will not be able to take part in the future Pi ecosystem. The new owner will also not be able to prove ownership of the account they received at big events such as migration. This is because of extra systems for name matching and liveness checks (checks that confirm the person is really there).

PiFest Promotes Uses in Local Commerce on Open Network

PiFest is a chance to celebrate the new ease of access of Open Network. It is also a chance to grow real adoption of Pi on a global scale. Pioneers do everyday deals and commerce with local shops that accept Pi.

From small businesses to large service providers, PiFest keeps connecting the community. It gives Pioneers the power to take part in real Pi commerce and to build uses inside the ecosystem (all the related apps and services). Whether you are a shop that wants to reach new customers, or a Pioneer who wants to explore the growing world of shopping with Pi, PiFest is a chance to take part.

The shop registration and event dates for Pi Day 2025 PiFest are as follows.

  • Shop registration opens: Wednesday, March 12, 2025
  • PiFest begins: Pi Day, March 14, 2025
  • PiFest ends: Friday, March 21, 2025

For event details and how local shops can take part, you can read the PiFest registration guide.

New Feature: Full Phone Number Change for Pioneers Who Have Passed KYC

Pioneers who have passed KYC can now update their full phone number after finishing a liveness check test.

Before, Pioneers who registered an account with a wrong phone number or an old phone number had no way to edit the full phone number. Letting Pioneers correct their phone number helps them log in, receive confirmation codes, and manage their account safely.

To prevent wrongful changes, this feature requires a liveness check test before the number is updated. The test confirms that the person asking for the update is the same person who sent and passed the KYC application.

Note: This feature must not be misused to transfer or sell accounts. Pi has a strict policy of one person, one account. As a result, if you knowingly give your KYC-passed account to another person, you will not be able to take part in the Pi ecosystem in the future. This is because you will not be able to verify any future Pi account. Future accounts will be marked as copies, because your identity and KYC information are already linked to your original account. Each Pioneer is responsible for their own account, the use of that account, and access to that account. So if you knowingly give access to an unrelated third person and that person uses the account for wrongful or illegal activity, you may be held responsible for that activity. Also, if law enforcement asks for information about the account, you will likely become a focus of their search and may face action by law enforcement. For people who receive or buy another person's account, they may not be able to use the account they received. This is because big future events, such as Pi migration, may need extra liveness checks or name matching. An account received from someone else does not have your name or your KYC information. So you will not be able to pass such checks before future migration, important network events, or benefits. Do not transfer or sell a Pi account under any circumstances.

Pioneers who can use this feature will see a Change option next to their registered phone number in the profile section of the Pi app. If this option does not appear, you cannot use this feature at this time.
