Grace Period Deadline Extended To 8:00am UTC On March 14, 2025

Published: March 14, 2025

Categories: announcement

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

As part of the continuing mission to ensure inclusiveness, fairness, and maximum participation in the Pi Network ecosystem, the Grace Period (the period with a deadline to finish KYC (identity verification) and Mainnet (live network) migration) received its final extension, to 8:00 AM UTC on March 14, 2025, which is the sixth anniversary of the project's official launch. The Grace Period deadline is the only Pi mechanism that has been extended multiple times. The extensions were made so that as many real Pioneers as possible can verify their balances.

However, with the six-month Grace Period and its multiple extensions, Pioneers should have had enough opportunity to verify their balances and receive Pi on Mainnet. So, to keep the network from moving into a new phase while holding a large amount of mobile balance that has been neither verified nor claimed, the end of the Grace Period is unavoidable.

Therefore, this is the last chance for any Pioneer to finish the steps needed to avoid losing past mobile balances.

Why the Grace Period is extended one final time

As always, this extension reflects the commitment to inclusiveness and accessibility across the global Pi community, including the many early Pioneers who were key to Pi's early growth. Like the previous extensions, this extension helps in the following ways.

  • Re-engagement of early Pioneers: because the Open Network launch renewed interest in Pi, we want to give people who were part of the early steps but became inactive a chance to finish KYC and migration
  • Inclusiveness and accessibility: Pi Network has always aimed to provide a fair opportunity for as many real Pioneers as possible to finish KYC and Mainnet migration. Those people ultimately form the foundation of the active ecosystem and the strength of the Pi community
  • Further relief for specific cases: this extension once again serves as overall relief for people in specific cases, and gives more time overall to finish KYC and Mainnet migration
  • Achieving the network's goals: while striving to be as inclusive as possible, the Grace Period is extended but continues to exist. This is because it is important for ensuring Pi's long-term success, by maintaining a real, verified, decentralized network

What this extension means for you

  • By 8:00 AM UTC on March 14, 2025, submit your KYC application and finish the Mainnet Checklist to migrate to Mainnet
  • The Grace Period rules do not change. If you do not finish KYC or Mainnet migration within the Grace Period, you risk losing most of your mobile balance (except Pi mined in the most recent six months before migration)
  • Now that the Open Network has launched, this is the final extension, so all Pioneers who have not yet migrated should act now

Next steps

To finish the Grace Period requirements, tap the hamburger icon at the top left to open the menu, then tap 'Mainnet', then tap 'Mainnet Checklist'.

You also need to encourage the members of your referral team and security circle to meet the requirements within the Grace Period. If members of your referral team and security circle do not finish the Mainnet Checklist by the Grace Period deadline, the corresponding bonus mobile balances that come from those people's contributions will also be lost.
