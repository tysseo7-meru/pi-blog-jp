Pi Ecosystem Summit: Save the Dates!

Published: November 6, 2026

Categories: announcement, developer

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Three days of developer news and showcases, and a $200,000 hackathon

Pi Network's first Ecosystem Summit is coming soon. The event runs online for three days, from November 17 to 19, 2026. It will include announcements of new developer infrastructure, technical demonstrations, conversations with developers, and showcases of Pi apps that present the utility being built across the Pi ecosystem.

The summit reflects Pi's ongoing work to improve the developer experience and to expand the tools and materials developers can use. The summit closes with the launch of Pi Next, Pi's newest hackathon (an event where people compete to build things intensively in a short time), with a total prize pool of 200,000 USDT. It creates new opportunities for developers and startups to build on Pi.

How to watch the Ecosystem Summit

The video program runs for two days and is streamed on Pi Network's YouTube channel, and a major hackathon launch takes place on the third day.

The first two days of the Ecosystem Summit are broadcast on Pi Network's official YouTube channel as YouTube Premieres (streams with a set release time that everyone watches together) scheduled at 8:00 AM (PST). Pioneers (people who use Pi) and developers around the world can watch the announcements, demonstrations, and conversations together.

Each day's video program combines Pi Network's technical demos and new feature announcements, developers' explanations of Pi integrations, and conversations with developers into one long broadcast. You can also hear directly from Pi Network's founders, Chengdiao Fan and Nicolas Kokkalis.

After each day's YouTube Premiere ends, both the full recording and the individual videos become available separately on Pi's YouTube channel. This makes it easy to look back at a specific announcement, watch an individual demonstration, or search the content at any time.

Save the dates and join the Ecosystem Summit

Readers are asked to put November 17 to 19, 2026 in their calendars and join to see what is next for building on Pi.

For the latest viewing information, links to the YouTube Premieres when they become available, and access to announcements, videos, and developer materials across the three days of the event, see the Ecosystem Summit page.
