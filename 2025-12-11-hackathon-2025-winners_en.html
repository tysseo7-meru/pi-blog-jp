Pi Hackathon 2025 Winners Announced

Published: December 11, 2025

Categories: hackathon

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

The winners of Pi Hackathon 2025 have been announced. The hackathon (a development event where apps are built in a short period), the first one on Pi in the era of the Open Network (the state where Pi connects with outside networks), called on developers to build and release meaningful Pi apps that strengthen Pi's real uses and widen the use of Pi. This was made possible by the Open Network's external connections. The winners are as follows.

  • First place: Blind_Lounge. A privacy-first social and dating space where people connect anonymously and reveal their identities only when both choose to
  • Second place: Starmax. A loyalty points system app where Pioneers can spend Pi at participating stores and earn rewards for their engagement
  • Third place: RUN FOR PI. A fast-paced runner game that builds Pi directly into the game's money flow

In addition, five apps were chosen as honorable mentions.

  • Kindrek
  • Workflet for Pi
  • PallyPay
  • SimpleJoy
  • Agora Pulse

The hackathon ran from August 15 to October 15, and more than 215 Mainnet app submissions met the hackathon's Mainnet (live network) ecosystem requirements. This shows a clear willingness among developers to build product-ready apps that focus on utility on Pi Mainnet. These hackathon apps are community-built projects that keep evolving after the two-month hackathon, so some features are still in progress, and occasional problems or limits may appear. Each team may polish its app based on user feedback and improve it repeatedly.

Pioneers can check all the hackathon submissions in the hackathon2025 tab, in the Mainnet section of the ecosystem screen in Pi Browser. Each winner and honorable mention is explained in detail below.

First place: Blind_Lounge

Blind_Lounge stood out for its unique approach to social interaction in the Pi ecosystem. By putting anonymity first and giving users the option to reveal their identity later, it offers a distinctive form of engagement that raises safety and lowers the effort of meeting new people.

The app's chat and dating experience starts without personal details, and identities are revealed only when both participants agree with each other. The Random Chat feature pairs users anonymously, creating a natural, casual way to connect before sharing profiles. The app also includes a social space where users can post thoughts, stories, and questions anonymously.

The team built Pi payments into the app's core features. By requiring a Pi fee when Pioneers create a profile, it screens out abuse and keeps quality. Every interaction requires a profile, and profiles are limited to Pioneers who have completed KYC (identity verification). These measures strengthen authenticity and trust, reduce misuse, and address one of the worries in dating interactions where you cannot see the other person. This app shows a good example of using Pi's strength — a large, identity-verified network — to solve a real problem.

In the words of the Blind_Lounge team: on large platforms such as Instagram and YouTube, people often suffer from false information and fake accounts. Blind Lounge, which only people who have completed KYC can join, provides Pioneers with a trustworthy place for real connections. It can become a meaningful meeting place that goes beyond simple online interaction and strengthens the bonds and base of the Pi community.

Second place: Starmax

Starmax provides a practical way for businesses and Pioneers to use Pi in everyday situations, through a loyalty points system that connects local stores and customers. Pioneers can spend Pi at participating stores and earn rewards for their engagement. Businesses can design and adjust their own points plans inside the app, engage with customers, and widen their customer base.

This is a system for merchants to attract repeat customers, offer meaningful benefits, and gain a chance to be seen inside a trustworthy system. Starmax's flexible points system fits naturally into existing store operations, and this app has strong potential to be widely used in the real world. It could become a tool for tens of thousands of merchants who integrate with Pi around the world.

In the words of the Starmax team: Pi creates a clear, easy-to-use environment where people can engage, trade, and grow together. Starmax helps Pioneers find businesses that are friendly to Pi, earn rewards, and support high-quality brands. For merchants, it creates a transparent growth driver based on rewards, gratitude, and trust. As brands grow, new Pioneers benefit, and the cycle strengthens itself.

Third place: RUN FOR PI

RUN FOR PI stood out by showing a clear, practical use of Pi in the money flow at the center of the game. It fits Pi's policy of valuing the gaming field.

Players can use Pi to buy power-up items, unlock levels, join prize pools, and compete on leaderboards. This creates a game cycle where progress and competition naturally involve Pi. The game has familiar runner game mechanics, a clean interface, and simple controls, so players can start right away and keep engaging.

The runner game type is well known, but RUN FOR PI's strength comes from using Pi in a way that fits its purpose. It ties progress, competitive elements, and the motivation to play again to Pi-based actions, uses the Pi community to gather users, and gives Pioneers an entertainment use. Game apps are also well suited to earning revenue in Pi using the Pi Ad Network, and later they can integrate further with gaming platform support for engagement and Pi utility.

In the words of the RUN FOR PI team: this game app aims to provide a real use of the Pi crypto asset in an environment where people can engage and have fun, through social dynamics such as competitive leaderboards. It creates hours of user engagement every day, and by collecting and using game items, it makes a meaningful difference for Pioneers. The final aim is to create a good cycle: games create utility, utility creates engagement, and engagement strengthens the whole Pi ecosystem.

Honorable mentions

Pi chose five more honorable mentions that provide utility in the ecosystem and advance the use of Pi.

Kindrek

Kindrek is a modern matchmaking and social platform that supports dating, friends, and work connections. It builds Pi payments and the Pi Ad Network into profile interactions, contact features, and paid elements. The project offers a polished, multilingual matchmaking experience. It stands out for building Pi into the social system, with the aim of encouraging meaningful, flexible connections for each type of relationship.

Workflet for Pi

Workflet for Pi is a productivity and collaboration platform built around role-based workspaces. Organizations can manage teams, projects, and customer interactions in a dedicated environment. Its ambitious scope and SaaS design (a form where software is used over the internet) show the potential of work tools in the Pi ecosystem. Its Pi integration is still early, and its reliability needs polish, but Workflet shows the intent to let organizations operate and coordinate inside the Pi ecosystem.

PallyPay

PallyPay is a group payment and bill-splitting app built for everyday activities such as meals, travel, and shared events. It enables small Pi transfers within groups of friends and communities, and makes coordinating shared expenses simple. The app stands out for showing Pi's usefulness in real situations where groups need to organize shared payments, and for exploring, from an early stage, business listings and group-based payments from businesses to consumers.

SimpleJoy

SimpleJoy provides a library of games that are fully open source, playable in a browser, and free and easy to use. SimpleJoy provides the Pi community with simple, fun, and convenient entertainment. The project stands out for its user-friendly approach and for answering a clear demand for light, accessible games that Pioneers can enjoy without effort.

Agora Pulse

Agora Pulse is a community voting app where Pioneers can create questions, share opinions, and vote on polls posted by others. The app is also trying Pi integration to support engagement. Its open, accessible design brings the mood of the community to the surface and helps draw attention to topics and opinions that matter to Pioneers.

Note that Pi apps are external apps. If you notice a problem or have a suggestion, please share it directly through the app's reporting channel or the related community places.

As a disclosure: to receive the prize money and prizes set in the hackathon rules, the winning teams and each of their members must follow all the hackathon rules and must successfully complete Pi's KYC verification process before receiving any form of prize.
