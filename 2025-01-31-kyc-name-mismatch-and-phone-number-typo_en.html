KYC Name Mismatch and Small Typo Phone Number Change

Published: January 31, 2025

Categories: general, update

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Two new updates have been released. If you take action, they may help clear a stuck KYC (identity check) application. These updates focus on the chance for genuine Pioneers to update their own information and move forward in the KYC process.

What to do if your name does not match

When the name on a Pioneer's Pi account does not match the name in their KYC, the application is usually rejected, because the person could not prove ownership of the account through the KYC check. This includes accounts that did not follow the requirement to use a real name, despite several clear notices on the app screen, and accounts that missed the two-week period to update the account to the real name.

However, as reports from the community show, genuine Pioneers can face this situation.

To balance (1) letting such genuine Pioneers move forward in the process and (2) the need to prevent violations of the terms and policies, such as transferring an account to someone else, an update was released that lets eligible Pioneers do the following.

  • Appeal and ask for a chance to resubmit the application with corrected details
  • Update the name on the Pi account to the KYC name, together with a penalty of losing part of the mobile balance

Eligible Pioneers will see, in the Pi KYC app, a text about this for KYC applications rejected over a name mismatch, followed by options based on that Pioneer's specific situation. Eligible Pioneers include people whose KYC application was rejected because of a name mismatch. However, a Pioneer is eligible only if they appealed exactly once for this rejection reason, and that one appeal was rejected.

If you do not see this text, you are not eligible for this feature at this time.

Note: the specific options available and the amount of the penalty differ depending on each Pioneer's specific situation.

Fixing a small typing mistake in your account phone number

To help Pioneers who may have made a small typing mistake when adding a phone number to their Pi account (and who have not yet verified that number), Pioneers can now edit up to two digits of their account phone number.

Unlike any other social media or internet account, the phone number linked to a Pi account is tied to that Pioneer's Pi mobile balance and Pi Wallet (the place where Pi cryptocurrency is received on the blockchain). It is an important proof of that Pioneer's uniqueness, and it helps enforce Pi's policy of one account per person. For example, people need to verify their phone number in order to fully pass KYC and migrate.

For this reason, updating an account phone number cannot be treated lightly. With this feature, Pioneers who made a small typing mistake when registering, or who entered the wrong country code in the past, can finally correct the number. Limiting the change to a maximum of two digits is meant to stop the phone number change from being misused by policy violators, such as people transferring or selling accounts, or by hackers (people who break in without permission) taking over someone else's account. Before any further feature allows larger updates to an account phone number, more safety measures will be needed, such as additional checks of ownership and identity verification.

Eligible Pioneers should now edit this through the profile settings in the Pi mining app. This feature is especially important for Pioneers who are in a Tentative KYC state and need to use the correct number to reset a password or verify their account.

Pioneers will see an “update” option in their profile settings only if they are eligible.
