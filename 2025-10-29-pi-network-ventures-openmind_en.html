Pi Network Ventures Announces First Investment in OpenMind

Published: October 29, 2025

Categories: announcement

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi Network Ventures has made its first investment in OpenMind. OpenMind is a company building an operating system (basic software) and open source protocols (rules) that let robots think, learn, and cooperate with each other. It is like an "Android for robots".

This investment in OpenMind fits the main aims of both Pi Network and Pi Network Ventures. Pi Network works to use the blockchain and the resources behind it to solve real-world problems. Pi Network Ventures works to bring more of the world's production onto Pi and to increase what Pi can be used for.

OpenMind's technology creates a shared intelligence layer that lets robots cooperate and learn together inside a decentralized system. This complements Pi's view of the future of blockchain and AI, in which a decentralized foundation supports open innovation and fair participation.

Beyond this investment, Pi Network and OpenMind worked together and completed a proof-of-concept experiment (a trial to check whether an idea actually works). In it, volunteer Pi Node operators ran an image recognition AI model for OpenMind, proving that Pi Node operators can run computing work for outside organizations. This early experiment, in which OpenMind's AI models ran on the Pi Node foundation, explores the ability of Pi's worldwide network of nodes to support decentralized AI training and computing work. This is another use of Pi Nodes, in addition to protecting Pi's ledger (the record book of transactions). It is also a foundation-level use that relies on the resources of the whole network, namely the computing power of more than 350,000 active nodes.

By turning unused computing power into productive work, this effort (1) lets AI products obtain and meet computing demand from Pi Nodes by paying the people who run nodes in Pi, and (2) lets people who run Pi Nodes choose to earn Pi from additional Node uses, separate from their node mining rewards. This use of Pi Nodes can help embed Pi further into the AI economy, through these computing and AI model training contributions.

The strategic fit between OpenMind and the aims of Pi Network Ventures

OpenMind is building a foundation that lets robots and intelligent systems interact with their environment and with each other safely, without depending on a single provider's system. Its hardware-independent OM1 operating system supports placing intelligent machines into real-world contexts. With the newly introduced FABRIC protocol, machines can verify identity, share context, and coordinate across physical and digital environments. More about OpenMind can be found at the linked page.

The investment in OpenMind offers a potential opportunity to work toward Pi Network's broader view of blockchain in an AI-centered world.

Pi Network Ventures' participation in OpenMind's funding round reflects its promise to support strong startups and businesses across the blockchain and technology fields that fit Pi Network's direction. This complements its three central investment aims.

  • To increase what Pi can be used for
  • To enable more of the world's production on top of Pi Network
  • To bring Pi into real-world use cases

About blockchain in an AI-centered world, Pi Network recognizes that technological productivity, especially from AI, raises questions about distribution, production, and the role of human labor and intelligence. In a future where productivity and transactions are increasingly carried out by AI actors acting on behalf of humans, built-in payment methods and ease of use for these actors are important features.

The foundation that lets these actors move autonomously in real environments and coordinate effectively will become essential.

OpenMind aims to contribute to the advance of an interoperable AI app layer, by building a shared intelligence layer that supports machine cooperation across fields, from autonomous logistics to industrial automation. This layer sits between high-performance AI foundations and real-world production, and in Pi's view, strengthening that layer is essential to make sure the benefits of AI reach human participants fairly.

The structure OpenMind is building may show one path for creating the coordination foundation needed to scale intelligent systems in an open and adaptable form. In turn, such systems may create opportunities for layers made possible by blockchain, such as identity, access, authentication, payments, and incentive systems. These are fields where Pi already has existing foundations and ecosystem activity.

In that context, the investment in OpenMind by Pi Network Ventures supports the broader aim of preparing an environment of technology and systems combined with real Pi uses, in a world that is becoming increasingly AI-centered.

The proof-of-concept experiment: building toward decentralized computing uses

The effort in which Pi Node operators can run computing work for OpenMind shows an early stage of exploring decentralized computing for AI training, using capacity distributed across Pi's worldwide node network.

Pi's consensus system (the way the network agrees on records) is designed to use energy efficiently. That means that protecting the ledger alone does not require all of the computing power of Pi's network of more than 350,000 nodes (although it benefits from the distribution across these many nodes). For that reason, a large reserve of unused computing power remains in Pi Nodes. This total computing power is a valuable resource that can enable new uses on top of the node layer, such as decentralized cloud computing.

As AI continues to require ever larger computing resources, using this spare capacity of a global Web3 network can help meet that demand and provide real uses for Pi. By letting nodes perform AI-related computing, Pi opens the possibility of a new field of uses and economic activity within the ecosystem. The side that needs computing power for AI model training can pay node operators in Pi for additional capacity, creating a decentralized cloud computing market that is easier to use and more cost-effective than traditional centralized foundations.

This idea may expand further by combining Pi's decentralized labor network with a computing layer. AI model developers can use both human feedback and computing power from within the same ecosystem. Through this, Pi aims to enable a self-contained environment where people contribute labor, provide computing, and receive Pi in return. This supports the next wave of AI development through distributed participation.

Note that the Pi Network Ventures investment is intended to provide both funding and strategic value over a broader period to partners considered best suited to create value in the world and provide additional ways to use Pi worldwide. The investment process is intended to mirror the ways of traditional Silicon Valley venture capital (companies that invest in startups), regarding procedures such as sourcing, selection, and review, and is designed to find and support high-impact, innovative startups and businesses. With that in mind, Pi Network Ventures' participation should be understood strictly as an investment, and it is not a guarantee that the invested company will succeed. The venture capital way seeks to support transformative startups and innovation. This depends on each team's strength and execution, and it focuses on companies whose potential for high success outweighs the potential risks.
