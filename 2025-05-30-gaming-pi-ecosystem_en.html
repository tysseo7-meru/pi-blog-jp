Gaming and the Pi Network Ecosystem: A Match for Utility

Published: May 30, 2025

Categories: developer, general

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Pi Network is building an ecosystem where utility drives engagement, and the reverse is also true. The goal is to support apps that provide concrete benefits to Pioneers, sustain developer growth, and make the whole platform stronger. Within that vision, gaming stands out as a field with high potential.

Why gaming fits Pi

Pi Network is a cryptocurrency and blockchain based on a social network. For Pioneers, it provides app-level utility such as games. For businesses and developers, it provides platform-level utility such as a large user base, collective attention, and the verified identities of millions of real humans.

For that reason, gaming sits at the meeting point of several important strengths that Pi Network is uniquely positioned to use.

  • Social engagement: Games are social by nature. Whether through competitive leaderboards, cooperative play, or community-driven events, games naturally touch the same kinds of social dynamics that make the Pi community strong
  • The attention economy: Games are a main driver of the attention market, providing hours of user engagement every day
  • Virtual goods, real behavior: In-game economies have already made digital transactions a normal thing. Players are used to collecting, exchanging, and using virtual goods

Pi Network Ventures and gaming innovation

Recognizing this fit, Pi Network Ventures considers gaming an important area to invest in, in order to raise Pi's utility. It actively looks for projects that bring in game design or full-scale games to advance the ecosystem's utility.

This means creative implementations of game logic, systems, reward systems, and social interaction design that make Pi available to more people, more often, and more useful.

Such games may do the following.

  • Bring in Pi payments for progress boosts or in-game enhancements
  • Turn social challenges, leaderboards, and cooperative play into further engagement
  • Use Pi platform features such as the Pi Ad Network, contributing further to platform utility on top of app-level utility
  • Create or hold special intellectual property (IP) that can be turned into game experiences

In this way, gaming is described as one of the excellent and productive paths toward creating utility with more impact, and toward connection with the real world.

FruityPi: a working example of Pi utility

FruityPi is a fruit-matching game that looks simple, and it is now live in Pi Browser. Players drop fruit into a box and try to evolve it into bigger, rarer fruit without letting the box overflow. But under the fun, there is something more meaningful: a demonstration of Pi's gaming utility actually working.

FruityPi builds Pi directly into the flow of the game. Players can use Pi to go further and build up higher scores. It also connects with the Pi Ad Network, showing how Pi's platform-level utility can support apps, inside the Pi app ecosystem, as a concrete, sustainable, and inclusive system.

The purpose of developing a game integrated with several Pi products, including the Pi cryptocurrency, the Pi Wallet, and the Pi Ad Network, is to show how a game can be built into, and published in, the whole Pi ecosystem. It is a form that benefits from what the Pi community provides: sign-ups, attention, engagement, and monetization in Pi through advertising.

The reason games are an excellent kind of app that fits the Pi ecosystem is that Pi is not just a cryptocurrency or blockchain project; it is also a social network. Game developers only need to build excellent, fun games and integrate them with the Pi ecosystem. The inflow itself and the business model are already provided with the platform. This is described as an encouragement for game developers to build on Pi.

In that sense, FruityPi is not just a game; it is a demonstration for Pioneers, developers, and businesses. It is an early sign that games, which naturally have social dynamics and virtual economies, can advance both app-level engagement and wider ecosystem utility at the same time.

Looking ahead

Gaming is a path for growth, experimentation, and real utility inside Pi Network. It is also one of the areas in which Pi Network Ventures looks to review and support projects. So developers are called on: if you are building a game, integrate it with Pi, use Pi products, and use the collective resources of the Pi ecosystem. If the game gains support from the Pi ecosystem, it should also apply to Pi Network Ventures.
