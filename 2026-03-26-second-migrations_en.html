Second Migrations and Referral Bonus Migrations

Published: March 26, 2026

Categories: pi-day

Original article

This page is a plain-English summary of an article from the official Pi Network blog. It is not a full translation. Difficult words and expressions are explained in simple words. For the exact wording, see the original article.

Since Pi Day 2026, Pi Network has continued the staged rollout of second migrations, opening a path for Pioneers to move additional Pi to Mainnet (the production network) and advance their participation in the ecosystem further. Pioneers who have already migrated their Pi balance once may be eligible to migrate their second migratable Pi balance.

As stated in the Mainnet migration plan, second migrations include referral mining bonuses based on the referral team members having fully passed KYC (identity verification). For that reason, Pioneers need to encourage their referral team to complete KYC. By doing so, these bonuses become migratable.

Since the release, as the staged rollout continues, more than 119,000 Pioneers have completed a second migration of their migratable balance, including referral mining bonuses.

While second migrations are rolled out, first migrations for eligible Pioneers also continue as usual. Second migrations do not harm the speed and throughput of first migrations. Furthermore, for the network to complete them, first migrations continue to be the priority. Pioneers who still need to complete their first migration will not be affected by other people's second migrations.

Second migrations required large-scale technical preparation. This is because the referral bonus calculation is complex, taking in the KYC status of referral team members, and differing for each individual and each mining session. In addition, because this work affects mining rewards distributed through blockchain transactions (transaction records), a more careful response and a strict checking and testing process were required to ensure accuracy and fairness. The successful completion of this effort is a major milestone, and it opened the path to the second migrations the community had long waited for.

Second migrations also coincide with a time when Pioneers can use Pi in ever more diverse ways. From Pi Launchpad, which is currently on the testnet, to the release of Mainnet functions for Pi App Studio apps at Pi Day 2026, new products and features continue to widen Pi's real-world usefulness and use cases.

Whether it is a first or a second migration, before becoming eligible for migration, Pioneers must complete the two-factor authentication (2FA, a system that adds one more step of confirmation on top of the usual one) of the Pi Wallet through step 3 of the Mainnet Checklist. This step is necessary to further raise the safety of the account and wallet before real Pi is moved to the wallet. The migration is a transaction on the blockchain that cannot be undone and cannot be changed. To complete 2FA, if a trusted email address has not yet been set, it may be necessary to add that email address to the account.

For the queue of currently eligible Pioneers, after the network has successfully completed the first and second migrations, Pi plans to start periodic migrations on an ongoing basis, with the frequency to be decided in the future.
